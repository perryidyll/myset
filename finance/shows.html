How these numbers are made

READ THIS ONCE

Which nights count

A night is counted when it started on a published gig — no earlier than 90 minutes before the slot, no later than its end — and something happened in it. Everything else is kept and labelled, never thrown away: unused (the gig was on the calendar, one phone, no votes — the room never used the app), or a test (nobody there; not on the calendar; ten or more phones on one network in under half an hour, which is one machine; under 30 minutes; over 12 hours with no gig). Hidden is a flag, not a verdict: a night the artist deleted from their own Money tab is still on disk, still judged by the same rule and still in the totals (the tracker cannot see the flag, and the two must agree) — it is greyed here and counted in the “hidden” figure.

How long a night is

The record's own length, unless the record overran the published slot (a show that ends itself adds three hours of grace). Then it is the later of the slot and the last song anyone started: a room still voting past the slot was still a room; an empty page left open was not. Two records inside one slot — an accidental End then Start — are one night.

People

Phones that opened the room, not just phones that voted (the app keeps the count itself; for the earliest nights, before it did, the peak voter count). “Networks” is how many different connections those phones came in on — the one check that a crowd was not one phone rotating its id.

Money

Tips, vote packs and paid requests are Stripe's answer for the payments tagged with the night, read when the night was filed and again on a re-check. A night whose lookup failed, or that ran with payments off, says unknown — never $0 — and stays out of every per-head figure. Untagged money taken in the night's window is shown beside it and never summed across nights. $ a head = (tips + vote packs + paid requests) ÷ phones, over nights with money known — merch is never in it. Merch is the app's own order record (an order is written the moment a session is redeemed, tagged with the newest night — “during the show” is counted separately), goods and postage apart; the artist ships it. The morning after a night the register asks Stripe once more, so a tip sent from the walk home still lands on it.

Where a night was

Country and zone come off the night's own record (stamped from the calendar gig since 25 Sep 2026), else the gig the night lines up with, else a venue in the registry with the same name, else the tail of the “City, Country” text older nights carry. Nights with none are counted in the total and flagged.

Counts the store forgets

The app keeps only the last 80 requests, prunes who-is-coming three days after a gig, and lets a phone replace its own rating. Since 25 Sep 2026 a night's request and RSVP counts are filed with it; for older nights the register keeps the first count it saw and never lowers it. A night that says “unknown for older nights” had its requests forgotten before either existed.

What the money model takes from here

Nights counted, phones and hours a night, votes and songs a night, actions per phone, and room money a head by plan — the same block tools/actuals.py used to print for pasting. The meters — ticks per phone-hour, credits a night, deploys — are Netlify's dashboard numbers and stay the tracker's; the model keeps those from its last reading and says so.
