How these numbers are made

Read this once.

Which nights count

A night is counted when it started on a published gig (no earlier than 90 minutes before the slot, no later than its end) and something happened in it. Everything else is kept and labelled, never thrown away: unused (on the calendar, one phone, no votes) or not counted (nobody there; not on the calendar; ten or more phones on one network in under half an hour, which is one machine; under 30 minutes; over 12 hours with no gig). Hidden is a flag, not a verdict: a night the artist deleted from their own Money tab is still judged by the same rule and still in the totals, greyed here.

How long a night is

The record's own length, unless the record overran the published slot. Then it is the later of the slot and the last song anyone started: a room still voting past the slot was still a room; an empty page left open was not. Two records inside one slot (an accidental End then Start) are one night.

People

Phones that opened the room, not just phones that voted. “Networks” is how many different connections those phones came in on: the one check that a crowd was not one phone rotating its id.

Money

Tips, vote packs and paid requests are Stripe's answer for the payments tagged with the night, read when the night was filed and again the morning after, so a tip sent from the walk home still lands. A night Stripe never answered says unknown, never $0, and stays out of every per-head figure. $ a head = (tips + vote packs + paid requests) ÷ phones, over nights with money known. Merch is never in it: merch is the app's own order record, and the artist ships it. Every dollar figure above zero is green.

What a show cost

Three columns, money going out, so never green. Server is the traffic the night added over an empty day, by Netlify's own per-day meters, at the price of the plan's top-up pack (every credit past the month's grant is bought in one). A night the meters were never read for carries the average of the nights they were, marked ≈. Stripe is an estimate at the published card rates the money model uses, on the room's money and the night's merch; Stripe's own fee is not filed with the night. No deploy is ever in a show's cost: shipping code is the founder's bill, not the room's.

Where a night was

Country and time zone come off the night's own record, else the calendar gig the night lines up with, else a venue in the registry with the same name, else the tail of the “City, Country” text older nights carry.

What the money model takes from here

Nights counted, phones and hours a night, votes and songs a night, actions per phone, and room money a head by plan. The meters (ticks per phone-hour, credits a night, deploys) are Netlify's dashboard numbers and stay the tracker's; the model keeps those from its last reading and says so.
