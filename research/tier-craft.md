## 1. The paid tier

**Headliner — $10/mo (or $80/yr).**

> Free runs tonight's show. Headliner gets you the next one.

The whole tier is built on one idea: MySet already collects the only thing a venue actually cares about — *proof that people show up for you and stay engaged*. Free gives you a great show. Headliner turns 40 great shows into a booking asset.

## 3. The one feature that does the work: the Live Proof EPK

Not branding. Not export. **The EPK.**

A one-page, always-current press kit at `myset.vip/yourname/epk`, generated from data the artist never had to enter:

> **Jamie Fox — Solo Acoustic, Koh Phangan**
> 26 shows · 8 venues · past 90 days
> **1,043 unique phones** joined the show · **avg 62 per night** · 71% voted more than once
> Most-requested: Wonderwall (34), Hotel California (29), Riptide (27)
> Best-performing venue: Coco Bar, Fridays — avg 94 voters
> ▶ audio/video embeds · past dates · upcoming dates · contact

**Why this one:** the artist's real problem is not running tonight's gig — they already do that fine, and free handles it. Their problem is that when they walk into a venue and say "I bring a crowd," they have nothing but their word, the same as every other guy with a guitar. Every other feature in Headliner makes an existing job nicer. The EPK creates a new outcome: a booked gig. At ฿2,000–5,000 a night, one extra booking pays for three years of Headliner. That is the only math on this list that closes itself.

It's also defensible. Anyone can build a press-kit template. Nobody else has verified, per-show, per-venue live audience engagement data, because nobody else is in the room during the set.

## 4. The upgrade moment

**Where:** the "Show wrapped" screen in the Studio, right after they hit End Show — the 3–5 minute window where they're packing up, buzzing, and looking at their phone anyway. Specifically, it fires properly the *fourth* time they see it, when the history wall first appears.

**What it says:**

>

>

> You have the answer in writing now.
>
> **[ Build my Live Proof page → ]**  Headliner · $10/mo · cancel anytime

Secondary wall, equally good: they tap **Share tonight's numbers** to post to Instagram. Free gives a MySet-branded card for one show. The upsell there is one line — *"Share the whole story, not one night."*

Third, quiet and effective: the bandmate wall. When someone hands their phone across the stage to change songs, the Studio shows *"Playing with a band? Give them their own login — Headliner."* No blocking, just a note where the pain is.

**What must NOT be the wall:** anything mid-show. Never interrupt a live set with an upsell. The Studio during a show is a sacred, ad-free surface.

## 5. Honest risks

**The seasonality problem is bigger than the price problem.** $10 against $50–150 a night is trivially affordable in absolute terms — but Koh Phangan empties out in the monsoon, and a musician doing two shows a week at half the crowd will cancel a subscription before they cancel anything else. Expect brutal seasonal churn, not price-resistance churn. Ship **Pause for 3 months** before you ship anything else, and lead with the $80 annual.

**The EPK only lands for musicians who are shopping.** A resident act with a locked four-nights-a-week deal at a hotel doesn't need proof of draw; they need nothing. That is realistically 30–50% of the base, and for them Headliner is band seats + custom colours + history — a genuinely weak $10. Don't pretend otherwise; that segment will simply stay free, and that's fine.

**A thin EPK actively hurts.** "31 people voted at a bar" is not an impressive number to a booker, and the first EPK a new artist generates will look like that. Gate it: don't offer the EPK until 5 shows and ~250 cumulative voters, and design the template to lead with *engagement rate* ("68% of the room voted") rather than raw headcount, which flatters small venues instead of embarrassing them.

**The history wall is what will get you talked about badly.** On an island where forty musicians drink at the same three bars, "MySet holds your gig history hostage" travels in a week. Mitigate hard and visibly: never delete data, always show lifetime totals free, and phrase the wall as presentation/export, not access. If a free user cancels, they keep everything they could see before.

**Removing "Powered by MySet" is a real growth cost, and I'd partly refuse it.** Every paying artist who strips the badge deletes an acquisition channel, and at $10/mo you cannot buy that traffic back. My opinionated call: paid gets full custom look — logo, colours, vanity URL, hero photo — but a small "made with MySet" stays in the footer on *both* tiers for the first ~1,000 artists. Sell "this page is yours," not "ours is gone." Revisit at scale, when the badge is worth less than the money.

**What this packaging structurally gets wrong:** it monetizes the artist's *career*, not the *show*. The highest-willingness-to-pay lever is a small percentage of tips and vote purchases, and you've ruled it out — correctly, for trust and adoption — which means the artist clearing ฿4,000 a night through your Stripe pays exactly what the one clearing zero pays. You are leaving the best revenue on the table on purpose. Accept it for year one; don't forget it's there.

**Five band seats for $10 caps ARPU on the highest-value accounts.** A five-piece pays $2 a head. Live with it — per-seat pricing on gigging bands is a bad look and a support nightmare — but know that bands, your most engaged users, are your least profitable ones under this design.

## 6. What it takes to build

Against what exists (profile with embeds, per-show history + stats + revenue, lyrics, Stripe Connect for tips, calendar in flight). Roughly **5–7 focused solo weeks**, in this order:

1. **Billing + entitlement — 3–5 days.** Stripe Subscriptions on top of the Connect integration you already have, one `plan` field, one `can()` gate helper. Do this first; everything else keys off it.
2. **Cheap unlocks — 3 days.** History window (a query limit and a UI state, not a data change — keep storing everything), CSV export, embed/link count limits. Ships revenue immediately.
3. **Live Proof EPK — 1.5–2 weeks.** The real work: aggregation queries across shows/venues/songs, a designed one-page template that stands up next to a real press kit, public share URL, and headless print-to-PDF. This is the product; give it the full two weeks and design it properly rather than shipping a stats dump.
4. **Vanity URL + theming — 4–6 days.** Logo, two brand colours, hero photo applied across vote page, profile, and lyrics sheet. Budget real time for contrast enforcement — the audience vote page must stay legible at arm's length in a dark bar no matter what colours the artist picks. Clamp, don't trust.
5. **Team seats — ~1 week.** Invite links, owner/member roles, auth changes. If auth is single-user today this is the sneaky-hard one; scope it after the EPK is earning.
6. **Calendar extras — ~1 week on top of what's in flight.** Recurring rules, follower "notify me" with email/push, calendar embed, Google Calendar sync.

**One sequencing recommendation that matters more than the build order:** do not charge the friend testers. Give them Headliner free for six months. They'll generate the show data that makes the EPK impressive, and the EPK is worthless with three gigs behind it. Charge cohort two, once you can show a new signup what a 40-show Live Proof page looks like.
