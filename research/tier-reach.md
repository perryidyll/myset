# MySet Marquee — $10/mo

## 1. The name and the pitch

**Marquee.**

> *Free runs the room. Marquee keeps the room after they leave.*

Marquee is the lit sign outside the venue with your name on it. It's the right word because it's about being *seen*, it's a musician's word not a SaaS word, and it doesn't imply the free tier is broken ("Pro", "Plus" and "Premium" all whisper that free is the demo).

The whole line is one sentence: **free owns the night, Marquee owns the week between nights.** Nothing at the gig is ever gated. The wall is at the end of the show and in the six days after it.

## 2. Free vs Marquee

Nine rows. The top two are load-bearing: a musician scanning this table has to reach "Same. Identical." before they reach anything gated, or the whole thing reads as a shakedown.

## 3. The one feature that does the work

**The gig announcement to your followers.** Not the city feed.

Here's why it, and not the obvious candidate. The city feed is the feature that *sounds* like discovery, but on the day you launch it is an empty room, and an empty room can't be sold. It only becomes valuable at maybe 20+ active artists in one city, and it will always be worth more to a listener than to the artist. Selling reach that doesn't exist yet is how you burn the trust of the exact 6 friends who are about to test this.

The follower broadcast is different because **it is generated by the thing the artist is already doing well.** Forty people scan a QR at Ugly Duckling, vote for an hour, have a genuinely good time, and then — today — they evaporate. That's the loss. Every gig a working musician plays is a room full of people who liked him enough to pull out a phone, and every single one is thrown away at the end of the night. Marquee is the only thing in the product that converts a night into an asset.

And it compounds in a way the artist can watch. At 15 followers nobody pays $10. At 300 followers across three venues, sending one message on a Thursday afternoon is worth more than $10 to anyone with a $50 door split — one extra person who shows up because of it covers three months. The number sitting on the free tier, growing, is the sales pitch. You don't have to write copy for it.

Second-order, and it's the part that actually retains: an artist who has 400 followers inside MySet cannot leave. That's true. It's also why the pricing has to stay cheap and the send has to stay unlimited-ish — the moment you meter messages or charge per contact, you've become the thing musicians already hate about Facebook Pages, and they will say so out loud.

## 4. The upgrade moment

**Where:** Studio → the wrap-up screen, immediately after "End show". Not a settings page, not a banner, not on the way in. Once a night, at the moment of maximum good feeling, with real numbers on the screen.

**What they see:**

> **Ugly Duckling — done.**
> 43 people voted. 187 votes. $24 in tips, $9 in votes. **All $33 is yours.**
>
> **11 people followed you tonight.**
> That's **214 followers on Koh Phangan.**
>
> ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
> │ **Tell them where you're playing next**  │
> │                                      │
> │ *Thursday, Amsterdam Bar, 9pm.*      │
> │ *Come vote. — Perry*                 │
> │                                      │
> │ [ Send to 214 people · Marquee $10/mo ] │
> └──────────────────────────────────────┘
>
> Your followers keep adding up either way. They'll be here when you want them.
> *Marquee is free for your first 3 shows.*

Four things that copy is doing deliberately. The message box is **pre-written from his own gig calendar** — the work is done, he's one tap from a thing he'd have wanted. The money line comes first and says *all of it is yours*, so the ask lands after a gift, not before one. **"They'll be here when you want them"** removes the hostage smell — the followers are not being held over him, and saying so is the difference between this feeling like a service and feeling like a squeeze. And the trial is **3 shows, not 14 days** — shows are the unit a gigging musician thinks in, and three shows is enough to accumulate followers worth losing.

Second, quieter placement: the Money tab, next to a real number — *"Announcements you could have sent this month: 4."* Once. Not repeated.

**Nowhere else.** No upgrade prompt while a show is live, ever. The Studio during a gig is a working instrument.

## 5. Honest risks

**The one real deviation I'd make, and why.** The brief puts "appearing in the city feed at all" behind the wall. I'd list *every* artist's gig in the feed, free ones included, and gate the *richness* of the listing instead (card vs line, follow button, live-vote link). A tourist opening `myset.vip/kohphangan` at 8pm to find music tonight must see all the music tonight — a feed with holes in it is worthless, nobody comes back, and then the paid listing is a premium spot in a place with no traffic. Gating the feed makes the paid feature worse. This is the rare case where being generous on free is the selfish move.

**The followers-as-hostage problem is real and I'm not going to pretend otherwise.** "You harvested my fans and now you charge me to talk to them" is a legitimate reading, and it's what Facebook did. Three guardrails, all non-negotiable: never throttle by follower count, never price per message, and never delete or hide a free artist's followers. Even so, one loud musician can frame this badly and it will stick. Have the answer ready in plain language: *the follower is free, the megaphone is $10.*

**Followers may be technically non-portable, and that's a structural lock-in you should admit.** The audience never signs in — that's the reason the app works in a bar, and it should never change. So a "follower" is a browser push subscription or a volunteered email, not a contact card. Push subscriptions can't be exported anywhere meaningful. Offer CSV export of volunteered emails on both tiers, and don't claim more portability than exists.

**The Koh Phangan audience gets on a plane.** This is the biggest honest hole in a discovery-and-reach pitch *in this specific market*. Two-thirds of that room is gone in six days and a follower in Manchester will never walk into Amsterdam Bar. Reach value here is concentrated in long-stayers, residents, staff and other musicians — call it 20-30% of a room. Before selling this hard, instrument it: measure follow-through per announcement for three months and let the number decide the pitch. If the honest answer turns out to be "announcements drive 2 extra people", say *that* — 2 extra people is still a fine $10, and an unfalsifiable claim is worth less than a small true one.

**$10 is easy for the founder and hard for everyone else.** At 6-7 shows a week it's 35 cents a show — one extra $3 vote pack a month covers it, and that's the line to use. But the median user is the guy playing twice a month, for whom it's $5 a gig with 40 followers behind it, and he will churn. That's fine, except it thins the very feed the payers are paying to be seen in — which is the second reason to list free artists' gigs. Expect conversion in the low teens and price the business on that, not on "thousands of musicians".

**What it might cost in word of mouth.** The failure mode isn't people refusing to pay, it's the wrap-up screen feeling like a slot machine after a good night. Show it once per show, never twice, and make it dismissible with the follower count still visible. And if deliverability goes bad — one artist blasting 400 people three times a week — every magic-link login on the domain breaks with it. Rate-limit to 2 sends/week per artist, one-tap unsubscribe in every message, and hard-stop any artist over a complaint threshold. That's not politeness, it's uptime.

## 6. What it would take to build

Roughly **three focused weeks**, on top of what's already live. The critical path is not billing.

**Already there and reusable:** Netlify Functions + Blobs, live Stripe with server-side verification and the three-path redemption in `_pay.mjs`, artist magic-link auth (`_auth.mjs`, allowlist in the `artists` blob), Resend wired for delivery, the Studio's four tabs, per-show history in `show.log`, and the events/gig-calendar work already in flight — which the feed and the pre-filled announcement both depend on.

- **Subscription billing — 2-3 days.** Stripe Subscriptions + hosted Customer Portal, and a `webhook` branch alongside the existing one. The trap: this is a *second, opposite* money path. Tips and vote packs flow to the artist; subscriptions flow to MySet. Keep the objects, the metadata `kind`, and the reconcile sweep strictly separated, or the Money tab starts reporting Perry's own subscription revenue as an artist's tips — the same class of bug as the $133 of unrelated charges.
- **Entitlement — half a day.** `plan` + `planUntil` on the artist record, one `requireMarquee()` helper, checked **server-side in every gated function** and failing closed. Client-side gating is decoration.
- **Follower capture — 4-5 days, and this is the real risk.** Anonymous fan id already exists (`myset.fan`). Follow = write to a per-artist follower doc — **shard it and read-back-verify like the fan vote records**, a popular artist's follow list will hit the same concurrency wall. Then: Web Push on iOS Safari only works if the site is added to the Home Screen, which will silently halve your reach and is not fixable. So capture **email as the primary channel** (one optional field, one tap, post-vote) and push as a bonus. Budget a day just for the iOS caveat and the copy around it.
- **Broadcast send — 2 days.** Reuse Resend. Per-artist rate limit, one-tap unsubscribe token, complaint/bounce handling, and a send log so the follow-through number in the wrap-up is real.
- **Claimed handles — 1 day.** `/a/:handle`, reserved-word list, case-insensitive uniqueness. Uniqueness must be a strong-consistency `getWithMetadata` on a `handle/<lower>` key — **not** a `list()` scan, which lags minutes and will happily hand two artists the same URL.
- **City feed — 3-4 days on top of events.** City normalization ("Koh Phangan" / "Ko Pha-ngan" / "Haad Rin"), timezones, and — unlike lyrics — this page *should* be indexed, so give it real SSR-ish HTML and OG tags.
- **Cross-promo rail — 1 day** once feed + profiles exist. It's a query.
- **Wrap-up screen + the paywall moment — 2 days.** Note `studio.html` is one `render()` with `if(TAB===…)` blocks and a bad edit deletes a whole tab while still parsing — assert every tab block and top-level function exists exactly once after touching it.

**Ship order for the 6 friends:** followers + wrap-up screen + broadcast first, all free, for a month. Watch what the follow-through number actually says. Turn on the $10 wall only once you can put a true number next to it.
