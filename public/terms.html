Who we are

MySet (myset.vip) lets an audience vote from their own phones on what an artist plays next. It is run by Idyll Enterprises in Johnson City, Tennessee, USA ("MySet", "we", "us"). These terms are an agreement between you and us. If you don't agree with them, please don't use MySet.

You must be at least 13 to use MySet. To make an artist or venue account, sell anything or get paid, you must be old enough to make a binding contract where you live (18 in most places) and meet Stripe's requirements.

If you're in the audience

No account. Your phone keeps a random ID so the page knows which votes are yours. Anything tied to it, such as votes you've bought and not used, stays on that phone and browser; if you clear its site data, we can't get it back.

Who you're paying. Tips, vote packs, paid votes, paid song requests and merch are sold by the artist or venue whose page you're on, through their own Stripe account. They are the seller of record and they set their prices. MySet takes a fee from their side of the sale; you pay the price shown. (On the founder's own artist page, MySet itself is the seller.)

Votes are final. Once you confirm a vote, it's spent. Votes you bought and didn't use carry over to that artist's next show. If an artist declines your request, or passes on a song you voted for, the votes come back to you.

Paid song requests. When you offer money for a request, your card is only held. You're charged if the artist plays and finishes the song. If they decline it, or the show ends before it's played, the hold is released and you pay nothing.

Refunds. A tip or a vote is a payment to the artist, and refunds are their call. They handle them in their Stripe account. If you can't reach them, email us with the show and roughly when, and we'll pass it on. Nothing here takes away a right to a refund that the law gives you.

Merch. The artist or venue sells, ships or hands over your order, and handles returns and problems with it. The shop tells you whether an item ships or is picked up at the show.

Be decent. Don't flood a show with votes by script, don't try to break or fool the voting, and don't post anything that breaks the content rules below.

You're the seller. For tips, votes, requests and merch sold on your page, you are the merchant. That means:

  • refunds, disputes and chargebacks are yours to handle, in your Stripe account;
  • taxes on your sales are yours to work out and pay;
  • merch must be yours to sell and legal to sell, described honestly, and sent or handed over as promised;
  • the gigs you list must be real, with the right place and time, because people travel to them;
  • if you take money for a request, play it or decline it.

Performing songs. Playing other people's music in public needs a licence, which is usually the venue's. MySet doesn't provide one.

The verified tick means a page passed our checks. It can't be bought, and it isn't an endorsement.

Paid plans

  • Plans are billed monthly through Stripe, at the price shown when you choose one. The free plan stays free.
  • Moving up takes effect straight away, and you pay the difference for the rest of the month.
  • Cancel any time. A paid month is yours to the end of it; after that your page moves to the free plan. We don't refund part-months.
  • Moving down never deletes anything. Features beyond the free plan switch off, and merch leaves your page until you're back on a plan that includes it.
  • Deleting your account cancels the plan at once.
  • If we change a plan's price, we'll tell you in the Studio before it applies to you.

Plans and featured spots are sold by us, so a question about one comes to hello@myset.vip.

What you post

It stays yours. Photos, clips, posts, diaries, song lists, merch pictures and messages belong to whoever made them. By posting on MySet, you give us permission to store it, show it on MySet, and use it to show off your page (in a share card or a featured spot, say), for as long as it's up. That permission ends when you delete it, apart from copies in backups.

Only post what you have the right to post. Nothing that is:

  • illegal, or infringes someone else's copyright, trademark or privacy;
  • hateful, harassing or threatening, or sexual content involving anyone under 18;
  • spam, a scam, or pretending to be someone you're not.

Venues, and artists on a paid plan, can hide posts on their own pages. We can remove anything that breaks these rules, and suspend or close an account that keeps breaking them.

Lyrics on the voting page come from LRCLIB, a community collection, and are shown as unofficial, for singing along in the room. An artist can remove them from any song.

Something of yours posted without permission? Email hello@myset.vip with a link to it and why it's yours, and we'll look into it and take it down if it shouldn't be there.
