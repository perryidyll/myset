The night

5 things happen.
4 of them without you.

1

They scan, and they’re in

One QR code on the table. No app, no sign-up, no email, no password — your fans never make an account. Print one pack and set them on the tables and bars at each venue. Simple as that.

2

They vote

Everyone gets a few free votes for the night, and they choose how many to put on one song. When they run out of votes, they can buy more 😉 — fast and secure through Stripe.

There’s a Lyrics button on the voting page too, so the room can sing along.

3

You stop guessing

One screen tells you what’s winning, how many people are in the room, and what they’ve asked for. One tap starts the top song.

Requests cost votes. Fans can add an optional offer — and you can always say no.

4

Money happens without you asking

Out of votes and desperate for one more song? They can buy a few. Had a great night? There’s a tip button with your name on it.

It goes straight into your own account, through Stripe.

5

And afterwards, they can find you

Everyone who voted was on a page with your name at the top. It holds your music, your links, your merch and every show you’ve got coming — and a Book button for anyone who wants to hire you.

Your gigs land in a public what’s-on feed for your town, so people looking for something to do tonight find you.

What actually changes

It’s not really about the votes.

Three things get easier the first night you use it, and they’re the three that make the difference between a gig you got through and a gig you loved.

Ease

The gap between songs stops being a decision. You look down, you see what the room wants, you play it. The background voice wondering “am I losing them?” vanishes.

Why: the queue is ranked live, and one tap starts the winner.

Excitement

Crowds feel more connected to a show when they get to play a part in shaping it. And everyone loves having more control. Fans cheer when their pick wins. They watch to see if it does. You feel it from the first chord — and playing to people who are with you is a completely different job.

Why: they voted. Nobody’s neutral about a thing they picked.

Freedom

You don’t just walk out with more money but more proof — how many people were there, what they wanted, what they paid. You stop hoping the right venue notices you and start choosing which ones you want.

Why: every night is recorded, and it’s yours to show.

Straight answers

The things you’re actually wondering.

Do my fans have to download anything?
No — and they never will. They scan a code, a web page opens, they vote. No app, no account, no email, no password. Anything more than “scan and tap” doesn’t happen in a bar.
What if nobody votes?
Then you play what you were going to play anyway, and nothing is worse than before. In practice one table starts, the rest see them doing it, and it spreads.
What if they request something I can’t play?
Requests are off until you turn them on, and they cost votes. When one comes in you can add it, mark it done, or decline it — so you’re never the villain.
Will it work on the venue’s terrible wifi?
Everyone votes from their own phone. The page is tiny, it backs off politely when the room goes quiet, and if a phone drops out it picks up where it left off.
I play originals. Is this only for covers?
It’s your setlist — originals, covers, whatever you’ve got. Voting on originals is arguably better: you find out which of your own songs a room full of strangers actually reaches for.
How long does setting up actually take?
About two minutes to a working page: your email, a six-digit code, your name. Add songs one at a time, paste a list, import a CSV, or pull in a public Spotify playlist.
Is the shop or the business dashboard free?
They come with the paid plans. Everything the room sees — the voting, the lyrics, tips, your page and your link — is free, and stays free. The Studio shows you the plans when you get there.
What does my link look like?
myset.vip/yourname — you pick the name when you sign up, and your shop sits at myset.vip/yourname/shop. Both work the moment they exist, on any phone, with nothing to install.
Can a venue or a booker contact me through the page?
Yes. The Book button opens a short form — their name, their email, what they want. It lands in your Studio under Messages, you answer from there, and nobody sees anybody’s email address.
