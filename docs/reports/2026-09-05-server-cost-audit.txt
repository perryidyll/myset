What one gig actually costs on Netlify.

ONE GIG

  as the ladder actually behaves (20 phones, 3 hours)
    20 phones x 3h -> 6,030 polls (302 each, one every 36s of gig)
    6,200 web requests | 16.3 MB | 15.8 function-minutes
    = 4.198 credits  = $0.0420 (Personal) / $0.0280 (Pro)

  if everyone stares at it (60% screen-on)
    20 phones x 3h -> 15,508 polls (775 each, one every 14s of gig)
    15,678 web requests | 40.3 MB | 40.3 function-minutes
    = 10.654 credits  = $0.1065 (Personal) / $0.0710 (Pro)

  absolute worst case: every screen on the whole gig, very busy room
    20 phones x 3h -> 27,372 polls (1369 each, one every 8s of gig)
    27,652 web requests | 70.4 MB | 71.4 function-minutes
    = 18.839 credits  = $0.1884 (Personal) / $0.1256 (Pro)

WHAT A MONTH LOOKS LIKE

  Perry alone, ~5 gigs a week             20 gigs =        84 credits  = $     0.56/mo of gig traffic
  10 artists                             200 gigs =       840 credits  = $     5.60/mo of gig traffic
  100 artists                          2,000 gigs =     8,396 credits  = $    55.97/mo of gig traffic
  1,000 artists                       20,000 gigs =    83,958 credits  = $   559.75/mo of gig traffic
  10,000 artists — the $10/mo SaaS   200,000 gigs =   839,576 credits  = $ 5,597.45/mo of gig traffic

  For scale: 104 production deploys in the last billing period = 1,560 credits.
  That is 372 gigs' worth of audience traffic, spent on shipping code.
