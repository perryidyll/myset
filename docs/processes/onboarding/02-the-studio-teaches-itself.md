# The Studio teaches itself: tip decks and the practice round

**Who:** an artist with a new account (role *Artist*) — or the act a sample page was built for, in its look-only Studio — and the Studio itself on their phone (role *MySet server*, which the map uses for everything MySet does by itself). The Venue Studio has the decks too, without the practice round. **Trigger:** the first-run ends, or a tab opens for the first time. **Outcome:** every tab has said what it is for, once, in a short carousel; and before a real room ever sees the page, the artist has run a pretend night on the real Live tab — votes, money, a request, Start, End current song, End the show — without a byte reaching the server.

**Every step is `Testing`** (CONVENTIONS: in the working tree, not pushed). Built in the working tree (branch `feat/sample-profiles`, 2026-09-28) — committed on the branch, not deployed. A step goes `Live` when it runs on myset.vip.

**A deck** is a centred card: a small moving picture in the brand's colours, one sentence, arrows, dots, a swipe, Esc, and on the longer ones a Skip; the last slide can carry an action. The words keep rules that `test/tipdecks.mjs` holds: one short sentence a slide, a few slides at most for a tab, a paid feature names its plan so a free account is never shown a door it cannot open (INVARIANT 0bx), and no slide promises what the product does not do.

## What is deliberately absent

- No coach marks pointing at single controls: a deck is a card in the middle of the screen, so a redesign cannot leave an arrow pointing at nothing (decision 0102, option B).
- No practice show on the server: a second kind of show would have to be kept out of every money and history path (option C).
- No Settings switch to silence the decks: an established artist is never shown one unasked, and the ? is the only door.
