# The business dashboard (pay, band, costs, hours, the report)

**Who:** the account **owner** on Bar Star or Rock Star (a member seat is refused with 403; a free plan with 402). **Trigger:** Studio → Money; *Log tonight* under the last show; a gig's *The business side*; *Report*. **Outcome:** every show carries what the act was paid, who in the band got what, cash tips, merch sold, costs, four kinds of time and the gear; the tab shows profit, the revenue mix, hours and two honest hourly rates for any period; a paper-white branded report for any date range or hand-picked shows. Decision 0065.

The rules that shape every screen: **profit is (pay − splits) + cash tips + merch + app − costs, and the hero names MySet's fee in dollars whenever app money is in it** (app = the filed night's votes and tips before MySet's cut and Stripe's fee; the fee named is MySet's percentage of it, Stripe's is never recomputed — INVARIANT 5d/0dt); **a night whose Stripe lookup failed has app money unknown, never $0** (`source` on the filed night, INVARIANT 0ef/0fc); **a rule default is not income** — a residency's pay is listed for a past night but only counted once the night was filed or logged (decision 0031); **the hourly rate counts the whole evening by default** and is computed over the timed shows only; **caps are enforced against growth, not size** (INVARIANT 0s); **money is cents and time is minutes** everywhere but the screen.
