# Merch orders

**Who:** a fan on the shop page (`/<slug>/shop`, or `/v/<slug>/shop` for a venue), reached from the shop card on the community page; the artist (or venue) fulfilling by hand at the merch table or by post. **Trigger:** a tap on an item card on the shop, then *Pay* in its sheet. **Outcome:** a direct charge on the seller's account, an order row with a short **pickup code** that the seller works through in Studio → Menu → *Merch store* → Orders, a receipt on the fan's phone showing the same code, and **nothing about the buyer stored in MySet**.

Merch is a paid-plan feature for artists (the ladder in overview §2.1) and a venue-Pro feature; `merchAllowed()` is checked by the Studio, the checkout **and** the page — one answer, three readers, so a lapsed plan removes the items from the shop and makes them unbuyable at once. The community page sells nothing since 2026-09-13: it wears one card that opens the shop, drawn only when there is at least one item (decision 0064; INVARIANT 0fo).

## What is deliberately absent

No buyer data in MySet (fetched from Stripe when the seller looks, never stored). No refunds through the app (Stripe's dashboard). No stock counts — an item or a size is sold out or it is not. No cart — one item per checkout, *Keep shopping* on the receipt. No tax — the artist's, and the page says nothing about it. MySet takes no cut of postage.
