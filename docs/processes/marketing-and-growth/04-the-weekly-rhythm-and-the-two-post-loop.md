# The weekly rhythm and the two-post loop

**Who:** the founder. **Trigger:** a week begins with the Sunday schedule block done (Gig-to-content p10). **Outcome:** seven posts, each a cell in the matrix of *topic bucket × funnel job*, weighted for the stage the company is actually at; Monday's vote manufactures Wednesday's or Saturday's reveal at no ideation cost, and the comment section under every Attract post behaves like a MySet screen. Thursday is sacred: the buyer gigs Friday and Saturday, so *"try this at your next gig"* is only actionable on Thursday.

| r01 | Pick the cell | task | Person | Founder R | — | Every post has two coordinates: **bucket** (YOU PICK THE SET — broad-but-adjacent participation; READ THE ROOM — musician authority, frameworks not tips; CROWD LAB — proof and category creation; the CHAOS AGENT slot — protected R&D; and LIBRARY, the search-and-owned layer that runs fortnightly, independent of the daily rhythm) and **funnel job** (Attract, Nurture, Position-Expert, Position-Journey, Convert). Neither axis alone is a brief. The test for READ THE ROOM: would a musician who will never sign up still find this valuable? If no, rewrite. `src: strategy §7.1, §7.2` |
| r02 | Weight by stage | conditional | Person | Founder R | — | The funnel-job ratio depends on evidence, not dates: **Stage A Ignition** (0–10 real gigs) is Journey-heavy with **zero hard Convert** — hard Convert with no proof burns credibility; **Stage B Proof** (10–50 gigs) is the baseline ratio; **Stage C Scale** (50+) pushes Convert and lets the data position. The ratios are the table in §7.3. The stage is re-checked at the monthly review (Measurement x06). `src: strategy §7.3` |
| r03 | Monday — gather the vote | task | Person | Founder R · Fan I | — | YOU PICK THE SET, Attract. A choice set with stakes, a live-performance signal in the frame (Content rules n03), one deliberately contentious option. Sets the week's engagement floor and **manufactures a later post**. `src: strategy §11.1 Mon, §11.3` |
| r04 | Tuesday — teach a framework | task | Person | Founder R · Artist I | — | READ THE ROOM, Position-Expert. Buyer qualification: frameworks, not tips. Carousels are underrated here — saves are the strongest ranking signal for authority content. `src: strategy §11.1 Tue, §6.2 carousels` |
| r05 | Wednesday — reveal, or a second mechanic | task | Person | Founder R · Fan I | — | YOU PICK THE SET, Attract. Either the reveal of Monday's vote — the internet's answer, or the internet's answer against what a real room chose — or a second participation mechanic. Every reveal is a built-in reason for the original voters to come back. `src: strategy §11.1 Wed, §11.3` |
| r06 | Thursday — before the weekend | task | Person | Founder R · Artist I | — | CROWD LAB. Stage A: Journey with a soft convert. Stage B onward: **Convert** — *"try this at your next gig"*. The buyer gigs Friday and Saturday; this is the only day the call to action is actionable. Never dropped, whatever collapses (Gig-to-content p11). `src: strategy §11.1 Thu, §15.3` |
| r07 | Friday — the human | task | Person | Founder R | — | READ THE ROOM / NURTURE. Founder and performer reality; the on-camera half that lives on the founder account (Content rules n11). `src: strategy §11.1 Fri` |
| r08 | Saturday — the experiment | task | Person | Founder R · Artist I | — | CROWD LAB, Position-Journey: the experiment, the result, or the build insight — ARTIST vs AUDIENCE, ROOM REPORT once gigs exist. Never dropped. `src: strategy §11.1 Sat, §15.3` |

This is the R&D budget; protect it — but it is the **first** thing dropped when a week collapses. Posted by hand, not scheduled, so the founder is present in the comments for the first hour (Automation engine a06). `src: strategy §11.1 Sun, §7.2 slot 4, §15.3, §9.2 [6]` |
| r10 | Underneath — Stories and thirty minutes of comments | task | Person | Founder R | — | Daily Stories are the heartbeat: behind the scenes, polls, gig-day content, artist reposts — cheap, no algorithm risk, where the warmest people are. Plus thirty minutes a day leaving genuinely good comments on gigging-musician content — actual insight, never "great post". Slow, unglamorous, reliably works for a new account. `src: strategy §11.1 underneath, §6.2 Stories, §6.7 #3` |
| r11 | Pin the tally at two hours | task | Person | Founder R · Fan I | — | On every Attract post, within two hours: a pinned comment with the live count (*"Currently: [song] 41, [song] 38…"* — a format, not a claim). The comment section literally becomes a MySet screen. `src: strategy §7.6 #1` |
| r12 | Reply with video to the wrong answers | video | Person | Founder R · Fan I | — | The three best or most wrong comments get a video reply — free follow-up content, rewarded heavily by the platform; on TikTok, reply-with-video is the strongest under-used participation loop. Disagreement outperforms agreement, which is why one option was contentious. `src: strategy §7.6 #2, #4, §6.3` |
| r13 | Mine every comment | database | Person | Founder R | — | Comments are the language bank, the objection list and the research pipeline: harvested into the `objections` table and the hook bank. The engine automates the harvest (a01) once it runs; by hand on Sunday until then. Over twelve weeks the public votes are twelve datasets — the seed of the Live Music Index long before product data can build it. `src: strategy §7.6 #5, §11.3, §9.3 objections` |
