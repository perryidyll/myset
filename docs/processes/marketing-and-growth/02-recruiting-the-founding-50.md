# Recruiting the Founding 50

**Who:** the founder, with one artist at a time. **Trigger:** an artist answered the DM (Cold start m04, *yes*). **Outcome:** the artist climbs the activation ladder — rung 1 *curious* to rung 4 *first gig run* and beyond — with the two catastrophic variables of the value equation removed by hand: **effort** (the founder imports the repertoire) and **perceived likelihood** (a setup call and a dry run before anyone looks foolish in front of a venue manager). Fifty artists at roughly ninety minutes each is the highest-return time available to the company right now. Rungs 1–3 are entirely human and entirely unscalable, on purpose; automate only after the manual version is proven.

Mostly **Draft** — the programme has not started. `Live` only where the step is a product feature already on myset.vip that the founder uses as-is.

There is **no practice mode** in the product (§5.3 item 4 is unbuilt); the dry run is a real show with one fan, which is fine — it writes a past show that can be ignored. **Never film a first-time artist's real gig without this rehearsal** (risk register). `src: strategy §5.2, §5.3, §16` |
| f05 | Send the QR cards | document | Person | Founder R · Artist I | Netlify | The Studio already generates print-ready codes ("Codes to print" — for tables, the tip jar, the case) and a "show it big to print" profile code. Placement guidance travels with the cards: the QR is what protects *show started → first external vote*, the second most fragile arrow in the funnel. `src: public/studio.html Codes to print; overview §3.4; strategy §12.2` |
| f06 | Remind them on gig day | notification | Automation | Scheduled jobs R · Artist I | — | **Draft — nothing exists.** A gig-day reminder by email or SMS (§5.3 last item) closes the *time delay* variable. A generated Room Report card is a product feature to build (Automation engine a05) and is *exploration-only* in content until it is (Measurement x11, evidence rule 2). `src: overview §3.4 Money; strategy §5.2, §8.3, §13.2 rule 2` |
