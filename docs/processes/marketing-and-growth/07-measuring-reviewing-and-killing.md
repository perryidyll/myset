# Measuring, reviewing and killing

**Who:** the founder. **Trigger:** every week, month and quarter; and every time a number is about to be said in public. **Outcome:** one North Star — **activated MySet gigs per week** (a real performer ran a real show and at least one external audience member voted); every arrow of the funnel instrumented; posts judged only against their own funnel job; a hook, a format or a franchise killed after four posts, and a funnel job, a bucket or the Expert layer never killed on views alone; and no claim ever ahead of the evidence. *A thousand signups that never start a show are worth approximately nothing.*

All **Draft** — the rituals have not run. The evidence rules (x11–x12) are binding today and are marked `Live` because they already govern every document in the repo (AGENTS.md § Safety).
