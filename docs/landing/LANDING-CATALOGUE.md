# MySet — Landing Page Catalogue (`public/about.html` + `public/index.html`)

**568 atomic claims** extracted 2026-09-06.

## Head, meta, nav and hero

`1-311`

**Purpose.** Convert a cold visitor — a working gigging musician — into a signup in one screen, by asserting a single mechanism (the audience votes from their own phones, you play the winner), stripping the three obvious objections (cost, setup time, does my crowd have to install something) before they can be raised, and showing one phone screenshot as proof that the whole fan-side experience is a single page.

**Buckets.** `navigation`, `meta-and-social-card`, `design-system-css`, `in-source-engineering-comment`, `narrative`, `value-proposition`, `CTA`, `reassurance`, `screenshot`, `asset`, `accessibility-alt-text`, `caveat-mechanism (pricing/soon markers defined here, used later)`

**Design.** LAYOUT. Single 1080px max-width column (`--maxw:1080px`, 22px gutters, L38-40). Sticky translucent nav (L45-47: 82% bg + saturate(180%) blur(20px), .5px hairline bottom). Hero is a CSS grid, `gap:44px`, single column on mobile; at >=880px it splits `1.05fr .95fr` (L97-99) so copy sits left and the phone right. A `<hr class="hair">` (L309) closes the hero before the next section.

NAV (L276-284). Left: gradient rounded-square mark (26px, `--grad`, red glow shadow) containing a 3-bar SVG (bars at y=9/4/12 — short, tall, medium), then the wordmark "MySet" at 17px/600. Right: a muted text link "For venues" (-> #venues) and a solid accent pill "Get your page" (-> /studio). Below 360px the text link is display:none and the pill shrinks (L64-67) — a deliberate overflow fix documented at L62-63.

HERO TYPE (L84-96). H1 `clamp(38px,7.6vw,64px)`, weight 700, tracking -.05em, line-height 1.02, hard-broken into three lines with `<br>`; the third line "You just play it." is an `<em>` painted with the brand gradient via background-clip:text (L86-87) — colour is the emphasis device, not italics. Sub is `clamp(17px,2.3vw,20px)` muted, capped at 38ch. Two "big" pill CTAs side by side with flex-wrap (L89-95): primary solid accent with a 22px red drop shadow, secondary ghost with a 1.5px inset accent ring. Both scale to .97/.96 on :active — a tap-feedback affordance. Reassurance line is 13.5px `--faint` beneath.

HERO IMAGE (L300-306). A drawn phone bezel: 300px max-width, 40px radius, 9px of #111114 padding, an inset top highlight plus a 26px/60px ambient shadow, and a ::after pseudo-element drawing a 78x5px speaker slot (L102-108). The image inside is radius 32px, `height:auto`, `fetchpriority="high"` — the only eagerly-prioritised image on the page (the four later screenshots are `loading="lazy"`, L389/403/433/580). This hero phone deliberately does NOT carry the `.tall` class the other four use, so it is not cropped to 600px. Caption underneath in 13.5px faint, centred, 300px wide.

COLOUR/THEME. All tokens come from /app.css, not this file: `--accent:#FF375F`, `--grad:linear-gradient(135deg,#FF375F,#FF6B45)`, `--accent-ink:#D6003C`. Two `theme-color` metas (L15-16) pin the browser chrome to `#F5F5F7` light / `#000000` dark, matching app.css `--bg` in both schemes. The page inherits app.css's `prefers-color-scheme:dark` block, so the hero renders light by default — while the hero screenshot inside the phone is a dark-mode capture.

MOTION. `.up` reveal (opacity 0 -> 1, translateY 18px) is scoped under `.anim`, a class an inline head script adds (L36), so a JS failure leaves the page plainly visible (L262-266). `prefers-reduced-motion` kills the transition and clamps all animation to .01ms (L268-271). Note the hero itself carries no `.up` — it is never hidden.

RHETORICAL SHAPE. Nav -> H1 (the promise) -> sub (the mechanism, in two short sentences) -> two CTAs (commit / just look) -> reassurance (three objection-killers in eight words) -> screenshot + caption (proof that the promise is one screen). The screenshot is the section's only evidence, and the caption reframes it from "what it looks like" to "how little the fan has to do".

## The scene, and the interactive demo ("Forty people, and forty screens" + #try)

`311-360 (markup); the widget's runtime strings that render inside it live at public/about.html:729-775`

**Purpose.** Convert an abstract product into a felt experience: first a second-person bar-gig story that names the pain and shows the same room fixed, then a hands-on widget that lets the reader cast a vote and watch the queue reorder, so they "understand the product" before any screenshot or feature list appears.

**Buckets.** `narrative`, `problem-framing`, `mechanic-explanation`, `interactive-demo`, `sample-data`, `caveat`, `navigation-anchor`, `no-JS-fallback`, `reassurance`

**Design.** TWO SUB-SECTIONS, split by a hairline rule (line 309 above, line 359 below).

(a) THE SCENE — `<section class="scene up">` (312). `.scene` is a narrow measure, `max-width:60ch`, centred (CSS 113); `.scene .body` is 18px/1.7 (CSS 114). Four paragraphs in a deliberate before/after shape: kicker "You know this room" (313), h2 (314), two `.body` paragraphs of status quo (315-321), then one `.turn` paragraph (322-324) that is visually marked as the pivot — `margin-top:26px; padding-left:20px; border-left:2.5px solid var(--accent)` (CSS 115) — followed by a closing `.body` (325-326). Emphasis carried by `<em>` on "near"/"with" (320) and on the song title "Tennessee Whiskey" (324). NO image, NO stat, NO citation anywhere in the scene: it is pure prose.

(b) TRY IT — `<section id="try" class="center up">` (330), centre-aligned; `#try` is the destination of the hero's second CTA "See how it feels" (296). Kicker (331), two-word h2 "Tap a song." (332), a `.lede` (333-334) capped at 46ch and auto-centred (CSS 76, 80).

The widget is `.demo` (336): a raised card — surface fill, `--r-xl` radius, `--sh-2` shadow (CSS 119). Header row `.dh` (337) is a flex baseline split: uppercase tracked label "Up next" on the left, a tabular-nums counter "3 votes left" on the right (CSS 121-123). `.dlist` (338) holds four `.drow`s separated by 0.5px hairlines (CSS 125-127). Each row is: a 30px circular rank badge `.pos`, a title/artist stack `.m`, and a right-aligned count `.n` with a big tabular number over a tiny "votes" label (CSS 128-139). The #1 row gets `.lead`, which fills its badge with the pink `--grad` and a glowing shadow (CSS 131). Footnote `.demofoot` (354-355) sits under a hairline, with `<b>` in `--accent-ink` (CSS 146-148).

WHAT THE STATIC MARKUP OMITS: the four `.drow`s hard-coded at 341-352 have NO vote buttons. The `.vb` pill button (CSS 140-145, min-width 76px, `:active{transform:scale(.9)}`, `.on` = pink gradient) exists only in the JS-rendered version (about.html:751-752). An HTML comment at 339-340 states this fallback is intentional.

RUNTIME BEHAVIOUR (about.html:729-775): local `SEED` array of 4 songs (734-739); local state `rows / mine / left = 3` (740); `draw()` re-sorts by votes desc then title (745) and rewrites `#dlist`, `#dcount` and `#dfoot` on every change; a click on `.vb` toggles the vote, bumps/un-bumps the count with a 380ms `scale(1.35)` animation (750, 770-772), and fires `navigator.vibrate(14)` (773). The footnote is one of THREE different strings depending on state (757-761). Whole section is inside `.up`, revealed on scroll by the IntersectionObserver at 784-792 with a no-JS fallback at 778-783.

ASSETS: none. The section references zero images — an unusual choice made explicit in the JS comment at 731-733 ("The real interaction, not a video ... which is why this sits above every screenshot on the page"). Nothing here can go stale by file date; equally, nothing here is evidenced by a real screen. For reference, the only images that exist are public/about/{vote.png, studio.png, fills.png, ask.png} (all dated Aug 31) and profile.jpg (Sep 2) — all used elsewhere on the page.

## FAQ ("The things you're actually wondering."), the 1-2-3 close, and the footer

`664-726 (FAQ section 664-704; CLOSE section 706-720; footer 722-726; the FAQ's one-open-at-a-time accordion script sits out of band at 806-812)`

**Purpose.** Clear the last seven objections that would stop a gigging musician signing up — friction for fans, fear of an empty room, fear of losing control of the setlist, fear of the venue's wifi, fear it's a covers-only toy, fear of the time cost — then convert immediately on a three-step, no-card, no-trial signup, with a footer offering the two other doors (public discovery, venue side).

**Buckets.** `objection-handling`, `mechanic-explanation`, `feature-list`, `social-proof`, `reassurance`, `numbers`, `CTA`, `navigation`, `caveat-absence`

**Design.** FAQ: section.tight, 52px vertical padding (about.html:71). Centred header — uppercase letter-spaced kicker "Straight answers" (.kicker :72) over an h2. Below it .faq is a single narrow column, max-width:62ch; margin:34px auto 0 (:238) — a reading measure, not full page width. Seven native details/summary rows separated by 0.5px hairlines (details{border-top} :239, :last-child{border-bottom} :240), so the closed state reads as a bare list of seven questions. Default disclosure triangle suppressed (:243), replaced by a "+" pseudo-element pinned right (:244) that rotates 45deg into an x when open (:246). Summaries 17px/600; answers (details .a :247) drop to 15.5px muted, 1.65 line-height, with bolded phrases lifted to --ink-2 (:248) — each answer carries exactly one or two visually emphasised phrases ("off until you turn them on", "setlist", "That is the single most important design decision in MySet"). All seven start CLOSED, which means every claim in this section is hidden on first paint — the objections are opt-in, and a scanner sees only the seven question strings.
