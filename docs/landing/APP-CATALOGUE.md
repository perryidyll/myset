## The audience voting page (public/vote.html, served at /:slug/vote)

**Files:** `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/vote.html`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/app.css`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/pull.js`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/sw.js`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/manifest.webmanifest`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/artist.html`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/netlify/functions/show.mjs`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/netlify/functions/vote.mjs`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/netlify/functions/_lib.mjs`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/netlify/functions/lyrics.mjs`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/netlify/functions/_lyrics.mjs`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/netlify/functions/request.mjs`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/netlify/functions/_requests.mjs`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/netlify/functions/gift.mjs`

This is the single page the room holds in its hands: one 1,164-line HTML file with no build step, no framework and no login, served at /:slug/vote (netlify.toml redirect), talking to six public endpoints. A fan opens it, is silently given a random device id in localStorage (vote.html:287-290), and can see what is playing, see the running vote board, spend a small allowance of free votes on what plays next, buy more, tip, ask for a song that isn't listed, ask for a birthday shout-out, and read the lyrics of whatever is playing right now. Everything the room experiences is free of plan gates — the artist's plan changes the numbers behind it (how many free votes, what a pack costs, how big the room can get) but never removes a fan-facing feature. The only true feature flag on this page is `voteFinal`, which defaults ON and makes a cast vote permanent for the round.

**Structure.** One scrolling column, max 560px wide, in four tiers plus a fixed dock. (1) Sticky header: artist name (tappable, goes to their page), "Live · venue · setlist name" or "That's a wrap", and a credits pill showing remaining/total or ∞. (2) A gradient hero card — "Playing now", the song title, animated equaliser bars, and a "Lyrics" button; when nothing is playing it becomes a plain "Starting soon…" card, and when the show has ended it becomes "That's all — see you next time" with a link to the community page. (3) A "Voting open / Voting paused" strip with the fan's remaining vote count. (4) "Up next" — the top three songs by votes, #1 marked "Winning — plays next", each with a + button. (5) The ask-for-something card (only when the artist enabled requests or birthdays). (6) Search box, a three-way sort bar (Top voted / Song A–Z / Artist A–Z), and a horizontally scrolling genre chip row. (7) "Vote for these" — the rest of the pool as rows with counts and a Vote button. (8) "Already played" — replay-cost rows. (9) Back links to the artist page and to /signup. A fixed bottom dock carries "More votes" and "Tip {FirstName}", and disappears entirely once the show ends. Everything modal is a bottom sheet that can be dragged down, closed with an ✕, tapped away on the backdrop, or dismissed with Escape (vote.html:811-870). Pull-to-refresh works anywhere on the page (pull.js).

**Design.** Apple-flavoured, warm, and deliberately calm. One accent — a red-to-orange gradient (#FF375F → #FF6B45, app.css:21-26) — used only for the things that matter: the now-playing hero, the winning position pill, a cast vote, the tip button. Everything else is white cards on #F5F5F7 with two soft shadow tiers. Full automatic dark mode (app.css:40-51, pure black ground). System SF-family type, tight letter-spacing, tabular numerals for every vote count. Motion is spring-based (cubic-bezier(.34,1.4,.64,1)): buttons scale to .9 on press, the credits pill "bumps" when your balance changes (vote.html:697-699), a voted button pops, confetti falls after a successful payment (vote.html:966-977), and `prefers-reduced-motion` kills all of it (app.css:190). Haptics on nearly every action via navigator.vibrate — 6ms on a stepper tap, 18ms on a cast vote, a 10-40-10 pattern on a sent request or a gift. A skeleton loader shows before the first payload lands and the content rises in once (vote.html:307-312, 685). Copy is conversational and never technical: "Winning — plays next", "No lyrics for this one yet — you'll have to wing it. 🎤", "Couldn't reach the room — try again". Touch targets are honestly sized — the sheet grab zone is 44px tall even though the visible bar is 5px (vote.html:236-238) — and the vote sheet's Confirm/Not-yet buttons are sticky at the bottom because on a 320×568 phone the rules list used to push them off screen.

### Traps for copy

- Votes are FINAL by default. `voteFinal` defaults to true in code (netlify/functions/_flags.mjs:34, since 2026-09-02). Any copy saying 'changed your mind? tap again' describes the OFF state. The page's own reversible-vote sheet (public/vote.html:484-496) is unreachable with the shipped default.
- 'They scan a code and they're voting' is one tap short. The only QR codes the Studio prints go to the artist's PROFILE page or to myset.vip (netlify/functions/qr.mjs:12-26, public/studio.html:2991-2995). From the profile page the vote link appears ONLY while the show is live (public/artist.html:389).
- Free votes are per ROUND, not per night. Starting any song wipes every fan's votes across all shards, so five free votes times a twenty-song set is up to a hundred free votes a night (netlify/functions/_lib.mjs:507-531; admin.mjs:1782).
- '100% goes to the artist' appears on the tip sheet (public/vote.html:936) but a free-plan artist pays a 10% application fee on it (netlify/functions/_plan.mjs:70; _connect.mjs:59-65). Plus is 2%, Pro is 0%.
- Card payments are effectively founder-only right now. canTakeMoney requires a Stripe key AND (the founding artist OR a completed Connect onboarding mirrored onto the show record) — netlify/functions/_pay.mjs:52-54, _connect.mjs:57, 284-289. For everyone else the buy and tip buttons open a 'not on tonight' sheet.
- Requests and birthday shout-outs are OFF until the artist switches them on, and the fan sees nothing at all when they are off — no greyed button, no card (netlify/functions/_lib.mjs:182-183; public/vote.html:733).
- 'Declining a request refunds it automatically' is true only for a request made during the CURRENT show. A request carried from an earlier show is deliberately not refunded, because its credits already refreshed (netlify/functions/_requests.mjs:167-176).
- 'The list updates itself every few seconds' is printed on the vote sheet for every fan (public/vote.html:455) but the server can widen the floor to 20 seconds in a 3,000-person room, and the terminal idle rung is 20× the floor — 400 seconds for a pocketed phone (netlify/functions/_lib.mjs:852-858; public/vote.html:1098-1105).
- 'Up next' can disagree with what the artist's 'Play the winner' button will actually start. The board is built only from songs not yet played (netlify/functions/show.mjs:76), but playTop's pool includes an already-played song that is holding replay votes (netlify/functions/admin.mjs:1791-1797). A replay can win without ever showing as #1 on the fan's board.
- The dock's 'From $X' figure is the minimum of the artist's pack prices AND a hard-coded 500 cents (public/vote.html:708). An artist whose cheapest pack is $8 has the page advertising 'From $5'.
- Search never filters the top three — the Up-next queue is built before the filter is applied (public/vote.html:587-592). A fan searching for a song still sees the leaderboard above it.
- Once the show ends the entire bottom dock is hidden (public/vote.html:704-705), so there is no way to tip or buy votes right after the last song — the exact moment a fan most wants to.
- In 'pre' status (account exists, show not started) the header still renders 'Live · <venue>' with a pulsing dot while the strip below says 'Voting paused' and every button is dead (public/vote.html:598-599, 575, 620-622). Do not screenshot a pre-show page as proof of a live room.
- The genre filter row is hidden entirely when fewer than two genres are in use (public/vote.html:550), and the filter is deliberately NOT remembered between visits while the sort order IS (public/vote.html:541-547).
- Lyrics are unofficial and community-sourced from LRCLIB, labelled as such on the fan's screen, and an artist can take them down for any song by saving an empty sheet (netlify/functions/_lyrics.mjs:1-14; admin.mjs:966-972; public/vote.html:887-892). Never call it a licensed lyric feed.
- A fan's identity is a random string in that browser's localStorage. Clearing site data, private browsing, or a second phone means a new identity, a fresh free allowance, and no memory of paid votes (public/vote.html:287-290).
- Being over a plan's audience number never blocks anybody. There is no head-count check in the vote handler at all, by explicit decision (netlify/functions/vote.mjs:81-92). The room slows down and the board shortens instead. And the cap is stamped when the show STARTS, so upgrading mid-set changes nothing until the next show (netlify/functions/_lifecycle.mjs:45-57).
- Nothing on this page is gated by the artist's plan for the fan — lyrics, requests, birthdays, tipping, buying, the board are identical on Free. What the plan changes is the artist's ability to set the NUMBERS (free-vote count, pack prices, replay cost, ask cost), which is Plus/Pro (netlify/functions/admin.mjs:1706-1708).
- The stepper caps a single confirm at 20 votes on the page and 50 on the server (public/vote.html:427-428; netlify/functions/vote.mjs:16) — 'as many as you want' is wrong.
- Unspent PAID votes carry to the artist's next show unless the fan gifts them; free votes never carry (netlify/functions/_lib.mjs:532-566). And gift.mjs computes the leftover without the fan id (gift.mjs:29 vs show.mjs:148), so an unlimited-granted device can be shown a different number than the one the ledger will honour.


## Artist Studio — songs, setlists, library, profile

The Artist Studio is a single dark, phone-first page (public/studio.html, 4,263 lines, 274 KB, one file, no build step) with seven tabs: Live, Setlist, Gigs, Money, Profile, Merch, Settings. This audit covers the two that hold the artist's material — Setlist (the song library, named setlists, genres, keys, private chord charts, room-facing lyrics, the songs-to-learn list) and Profile (name, tagline, bio, five photos, five streaming links, up to 24 video/music embeds, and the community-page moderation panel). Every write goes to one endpoint, /api/admin (netlify/functions/admin.mjs, 105 KB), which authenticates by studio code + page name and answers most writes with the whole refreshed Studio state so the UI does not need a second round trip (admin.mjs:2017-2020). The library is the single source of truth for what a song IS; a named setlist only ever holds song ids, like a playlist (_lists.mjs:3-7).

**Structure.** Seven pill tabs stick under a blurred sticky header (studio.html:2539-2547); the tab choice is remembered in localStorage (studio.html:1615). ### Traps for copy

- THE STARTER PACK IS 62 SONGS, exactly (netlify/functions/_lib.mjs:47-112, verified by running the module). The Studio's own confirm dialog and the current landing page both say "around sixty", which is fine; if you ever want a number, it is 62. It appends and never replaces, and skips anything already in the library by song id.
- STARTER-PACK SONGS ARRIVE WITH NO GENRES AND NO KEY. The starterSetlist handler pushes {id, title, artist, active} only (admin.mjs:1972-1984) — unlike addSong and learnDone, it does not call the genre map. So "add the starter pack" and "tagged and ready" are two different steps: the artist still has to tap Auto-tag.
- ON FREE, THE STARTER PACK IS 12 SONGS OVER THE CAP ON DAY ONE. Free features 50 songs at a time (_plan.mjs:20); the pack is 62. The last 12 arrive switched off, silently — starterSetlist sets active:false for them but attaches no note, unlike addSong and importSongs which both say so. Never write copy implying a free artist gets all sixty-odd live.
- CREATING A NEW NAMED SETLIST IS PLUS-GATED. admin.mjs:730-737 refuses listNew on free with a 402. USING, renaming, editing membership, deleting and attaching one to a gig all stay free. public/about.html:606 lists "Setlists, chord charts, keys and genres" under Free, and the FAQ at about.html:693-699 tells the reader to "build a setlist instead — name it, put the songs you want in it" with no mention of a plan. A free artist cannot do that. This gate landed 2026-09-03 (commits 9036f98 then 533b109); the FAQ has not caught up. The safe free framing is HIDING songs, which is free and permanent-until-undone.
- THE FOUNDER BYPASSES THE SETLIST GATE. isPlatformOwner (_plan.mjs:154) lets perry-idyll create setlists regardless of plan, and planForArtist returns 'free' for him because he predates the registry. So testing the free experience in Perry's own Studio will show setlists working. Same bypass exists for merch and post-deletion.
- "CLEAR SETLIST" EMPTIES THE WHOLE LIBRARY, not the active set. admin.mjs:1985 is `show.songs = []`. The button sits next to "+ Starter pack" (studio.html:1955-1957) and its confirm says "Remove every song from your setlist? This cannot be undone." Any copy that says "clear tonight's set" is describing something that does not exist.
- AUTO-TAG ONLY KNOWS 62 SONGS AND 76 ARTISTS. The by-song map in _genremap.mjs:25-88 IS the starter pack, one to one (all 62 resolve; verified). The fallback at :92-169 is 76 artists. A song by neither comes back with nothing and stays blank — the results sheet lists them by name so the artist can tag them by hand. Do not write "auto-tags your whole setlist"; write something like "fills in the blanks it recognises".
- AUTO-TAG NEVER OVERWRITES A CHOICE. It only ever fills songs that have no genres at all (admin.mjs:648), so running it twice is safe and running it after hand-tagging leaves the hand-tagging alone. That is a genuine reassurance and worth saying.
- THERE ARE 18 BUILT-IN GENRES, NOT 15. _lib.mjs:209-230 lists 18; the comment three lines above still says "Fifteen" because the three tempo tags (slow, mid-tempo, upbeat) were added later. Sing-along is one of the built-ins on purpose. Artists can add 15 more of their own, 6 per song.
- UPGRADING DOES NOT BUY MORE STORAGE. MAX_LIBRARY is 2,000 on free, Plus and Pro alike (_plan.mjs:133, shipped to every plan row at admin.mjs:399). What a paid plan buys is how many are LIVE at once: 50 on free, unlimited on Plus and Pro. The Studio's own line gets this right ("Keep as many as you like (up to 2,000) — your plan shows 50 to fans at a time"); copy that says "unlimited songs" without that distinction is wrong in both directions.

The chart ("only you ever see this", 20,000 chars, own document, never in any public payload — _chart.mjs:3-12) is for the artist mid-song. The Artist Studio is a single 4,263-line dark-only page at myset.vip/studio that a working musician holds on stage. It is one client-rendered app with seven tabs (Live, Setlist, Gigs, Money, Profile, Merch, Settings) driven by one `render()` function, fed by one read endpoint `/api/stage` and one write endpoint `/api/admin`. The night-running surface is the Live tab (voting open/pause, three stat tiles, now-playing, the requests panel, start/end the show, "Start top voted", the queue, the played list), the Gigs tab (a month calendar of bookings that also drives shows that start and end themselves), and the parts of Settings that price and configure a show. It is an installable PWA — that install is the only way an iPhone can receive the request alerts — and it is `noindex,nofollow`, so it is not a page marketing can link to as a demo.

**Structure.** Sign-in gate first (`gate()`, public/studio.html:959) — email code, passkey/Face ID, studio code, or a recovery code; there is no password anywhere. Past the gate the whole app is one `render()` (public/studio.html:1854) writing into `#app`: a sticky header (home mark, "● On stage" when live, artist name linking to the public page, and either an "Upgrade" button or a plan chip), then a 7-tab row that never changes with plan (public/studio.html:2539-2547), then the tab body, then a single big footer button whose destination changes by tab — "See what fans see ↗" on Live (the voting page) and "See your page ↗" everywhere else (public/studio.html:2548-2560). The chosen tab is remembered in localStorage (`myset.tab`, public/studio.html:1615). Only the Live tab auto-refreshes: a 4-second interval that fires only when the document is visible AND `TAB==='live'` (public/studio.html:4246); every other tab is static until you pull down to refresh (`MySetPull`, public/studio.html:4248). Detail work happens in bottom sheets with a drag-to-dismiss grab bar (`openSheet`, public/studio.html:2653) — add/edit a gig, the plans sheet, the setlist picker, the QR lightbox. Deep flows leave the page entirely to Stripe Checkout and come back on `?sub=`, `?connect=`, `?promoted=`, `?promocancel=` query params (public/studio.html:1013-1029, 3624-3640).

**Design.** Dark only, and deliberately — `--bg:#000`, `--surface:#1C1C1E`, `--ink:#F5F5F7`, accents `--accent:#FF456E` (pink) and `--accent-2:#FF7A45` (orange) (public/studio.html:26-32). There is no light theme and no toggle; it is built for a dim stage. iOS-flavoured: rounded surfaces, hairline dividers, big thumb-sized primary buttons (`.bigplay`, 20px padding, public/studio.html:113). A black boot screen with three animated bars and a line of copy that cycles every 1.4s through "Warming up the room / Tuning the setlist / Counting tonight's votes…" (public/studio.html:4192-4206), and a hard 6-second failsafe that tears it down whatever happens (public/studio.html:4242). ### Traps for copy

- THERE IS NO STAGE VIEW. public/stage.html is a nine-line meta-refresh to /studio.html (public/stage.html:1-9) and stage.mjs is the Studio's own private data endpoint, not a big screen. Never write about projecting the queue, a stage display, or a screen the room watches.
- The Live tab's third stat tile says 'Tips' and shows the artist's ALL-TIME tip total, not tonight's. `meta.tips` is appended to forever and never reset or trimmed (netlify/functions/stage.mjs:36, 80; netlify/functions/_pay.mjs:91). 'Recent tips' is likewise the last 15 ever. Do not write 'see tonight's tips as they land'.
- The Studio only refreshes itself on the Live tab (public/studio.html:4246). Gigs, Money, Profile, Merch and Settings do not update until you pull down. 'Your whole Studio, live' is false.
- The free tier's limit is 4 SHOWS PER UTC CALENDAR MONTH, not per week and not a feature limit (netlify/functions/_plan.mjs:33; _lib.mjs:131). It was briefly two a week on 2026-09-03 and was changed straight back. Free artists get the whole Studio — the lyrics sheet, the calendar, the community page, hiding posts, show history, 2,000 stored songs. What free costs is a 10% cut and 50 songs live to the room at once.
- The plan numbers are 200 / 1,000 / 2,000 people in the room (free / Plus / Pro), and they are SOFT (netlify/functions/_plan.mjs:63, 88, 106; show.mjs:32-44). Nobody is locked out, no vote is refused; the room polls more slowly and the board shortens. Pro was deliberately brought DOWN from 3,000 to 2,000 on 2026-09-05 because the arithmetic behind 3,000 was wrong — do not quote 3,000 from any older doc.
- The code comment claims the artist is told after a night went over the room size. That is NOT implemented — `room.over` is sent to fan phones and rendered nowhere, and the Studio payload does not carry the cap at all. Never promise an over-capacity notice.
- Four Pro features have no code behind them anywhere: promote, analytics, presskit, branding (netlify/functions/_plan.mjs:130). They are shown in the Studio as 'Coming soon' even to Pro and even to the founder. Pro's own plan card words it correctly as 'Coming soon, included'. Do not sell earnings-by-venue, a press kit, custom branding or cross-city promotion as things Pro gets today.
- 'Promote a gig' ($10 for one of three featured spots in a city, per night) IS live today, but it is behind the `featuredShows` feature flag with a default of ON and an explicit removal plan: after a month, if nobody buys, the flag AND the feature get deleted (netlify/functions/_flags.mjs:38-49). - Requests and birthday shout-outs are OFF until the artist switches each one on (netlify/functions/_requests.mjs:12-15), and the switches are in Settings while the results appear on the Live tab. Switching them ON is free on every plan; only setting what they COST is a Plus feature. Do not say 'requests are built in and on'.

### Traps for copy

- THERE ARE TWO DIFFERENT KINDS OF 'LIVE' ON THE ARTIST PAGE AND THEY DISAGREE. The pink "Live now — vote the setlist" pill on the cover comes from the artist's SHOW record (show.status === 'live', profile.mjs:38) — the artist has to have started a show in the Studio, or had one auto-start. The "On now" badge in the Upcoming shows list comes purely from the CALENDAR (events.mjs:160 — now is between startsAt and endsAt). So a page can say "On now" on a gig row while showing no Live pill and no Join live button, and vice versa. Never write copy that treats them as one state.
- THE COUNTDOWN DOES NOT COUNT DOWN. The label is correct when the page loads and then freezes until the page is reloaded — startTick() bails out on line 334 of artist.html because it tests `S`, an object that was removed from boot() on 2026-09-04 (commit 67f1d36) and is now hardcoded null. about.html's own image alt text already says "a live countdown to the next gig", which overstates it. Safe phrasing: "shows how long until the next gig". Unsafe: "a live ticking countdown".
- 'FANS' IS NOT UNIQUE PEOPLE. It is the sum, across every archived show, of how many phones were in the room that night (profile.mjs:20). The same regular at ten gigs counts ten times. It also counts a phone that merely opened the voting page and stamped presence, not just people who voted (_lib.mjs:752-755). Honest phrasings: "people in the room, added up across every night", "total attendance". Dishonest: "your fanbase", "unique fans", "people who follow you". There is no follow feature at all (profile.mjs:12).
- 'SHOWS' COUNTS NIGHTS MYSET WAS USED, NOT GIGS PLAYED. It is the length of the archived-show index (profile.mjs:15). A night that was never started, or that had no song, no vote and nobody in the room, is refused by the archiver and never appears (_history.mjs:107-123). So an artist who plays four gigs but only runs MySet at two shows "2". Do not say "every gig you've played".
- A STAT TILE WITH A ZERO VANISHES ENTIRELY, it does not show 0 (artist.html:413, compact() returns '' for 0 at line 302). A brand-new artist's page shows Joined, possibly Songs, and the Community button — three cells in a row built for six. Any screenshot or mockup showing five populated tiles is showing an experienced account.
- 'SONGS' IS THE LIVE COUNT, AND ON THE FREE PLAN IT CANNOT EXCEED 50. It counts library songs with active !== false (profile.mjs:32); the free plan's `featured` limit is 50 songs live to the audience at once (_plan.mjs:20), while everyone can STORE up to 2,000 (_plan.mjs:133). "Your whole library on your page" would be wrong for a free artist with 300 songs.
- THE VERIFIED TICK IS THE ONLY PLAN-GATED THING ON THIS ENTIRE PUBLIC SURFACE, and it needs BOTH the registry flag and a currently-paid plan — `verified && planOf(who) !== 'free'` (profile.mjs:27). A comped plan that lapses drops the tick on the next page load with nothing firing. And the framing in the code is precise and worth keeping: paying opens the door to being CHECKED, it does not buy the tick (_verify.mjs:185-188).
- THE ARTIST TICK AND THE VENUE TICK ARE EARNED COMPLETELY DIFFERENTLY. A venue's needs a paid plan, a sign-in email on the venue's own domain, the venue's website naming the venue, and three different artists with gigs there vouching (_verify.mjs:7-30, 204-205). An artist's needs a paid plan, working Stripe card payments, a photo of an ID with a legal name and date of birth, and either Stripe's identity check matching or Perry looking at it (_verify.mjs:314-328). Same green chip, same glyph, two totally different stories.
- THE VERIFIED TICK DOES NOT APPEAR IN THE CITY FEED. The feed rows carry only name, slug, venue, times and address (events.mjs:154-161, 80-85). The tick exists on the artist page, the community page and the venue page only. Do not imply a verified act stands out in the listings.
- 'PROMOTE' MEANS TWO DIFFERENT THINGS IN THIS CODEBASE AND ONLY ONE IS REAL. The Pro plan's `promote` flag ("list gigs in cities you don't normally play") is in NOT_BUILT — there is no code behind it anywhere (_plan.mjs:74, 112-130). The $10 Featured Shows spot IS built and shipped, is gated only by a feature flag, and is available to artists on ANY plan including free — handleFeature checks the flag and Stripe and nothing else (admin.mjs:1404-1470). Never write "featured listings are a Pro feature".
- FEATURED SHOWS SITS BEHIND A FEATURE FLAG THAT DEFAULTS ON BUT CAN BE SWITCHED OFF GLOBALLY AT ANY MOMENT (_flags.mjs:38-49). With it off there is no Promote button, the endpoint refuses so nobody can be charged, and the Featured section is not rendered — but spots already paid for keep their record and reappear the moment it comes back. It was shipped 2026-09-05 as an experiment Perry wanted to test, with an explicit removal plan: if nobody buys, delete the flag AND the feature.
- CANCELLING A PROMOTED GIG SPENDS THE $10. The feed renders a featured row only when the occurrence still exists, so deleting the gig makes the paid spot silently stop appearing and there is no refund (events.mjs:130, _featured.mjs:36-38). The refund path only exists for a payment that lands after the hold expired and the night filled, or a duplicate purchase.
- THE GIG COUNTS IN THE COUNTRY/CITY PICKER ONLY LOOK AT THE FIRST 40 OWNERS IN A CITY (events.mjs:181, `ids.slice(0, 40)`), while the actual feed loops over every owner with no cap (events.mjs:68). In a busy city the number beside the city name will UNDER-count what the feed then shows. Do not describe the picker counts as exact.
- THE FEED IS SEVEN DAYS, HARD-CODED (WINDOW_DAYS = 7, events.mjs:8). The artist page's own list looks 90 days ahead but the server returns at most 60 occurrences and the page renders at most 24, with only 3 visible until "See N more" is tapped (artist.html:359, 439, 452-456; events.mjs:44-50). "All your upcoming gigs" is a stretch; "the next three months" is closer.
- AN ARTIST ONLY APPEARS IN A CITY'S FEED IF THE GIG HAS BOTH A COUNTRY AND A CITY FILLED IN (_events.mjs:180-181). And a ONE-OFF gig only keeps them listed until seven days after it happened, whereas a REPEATING gig keeps them in that city indefinitely (_events.mjs:182-185). "Add a gig and you're in your town's listings" needs the city caveat.
- 'TONIGHT' AND 'TOMORROW' ARE COMPUTED IN DIFFERENT TIMEZONES ON THE TWO PAGES. The city feed labels days in the GIG's timezone, and groups a whole city's night by the first row's timezone (events.mjs:89-92, 169-175). The artist page computes them from the VIEWER's own clock (artist.html:289-297). A traveller can see the same gig labelled differently on the two pages.
- SHARING AN ARTIST LINK GIVES A GENERIC PREVIEW CARD. artist.html's OpenGraph tags are static — og:title is just "MySet", og:description is "Live music. You pick what gets played.", og:image is the stock /img/band.jpg (artist.html:21-23). The artist's name and photo are only set in JavaScript after load (line 495), which link unfurlers do not run. Do not claim "share your page and your photo shows up".
- EVERY ARTIST WITHOUT PHOTOS GETS THE SAME STOCK BAND PICTURE. /img/band.jpg is the default for both the cover AND the avatar (_profile.mjs:50; artist.html:387, 395). A page that has never been filled in still looks furnished — which is good for screenshots and misleading in a demo.
- THE ARTIST PAGE SELLS NOTHING. No merch, no tips, no ticket sales through MySet. /api/profile returns a merch count but artist.html never reads it — merch lives on the /:slug/community page and is a Plus/Pro feature (_plan.mjs:68, 90, 108). "Tickets ↗" on a gig row is just an outbound link to whatever URL the artist pasted (artist.html:450).
- THERE IS NO GLOBAL ARTIST SEARCH. The only way to reach an artist page from the home page is to pick their country, pick their city, and have them playing there within seven days (index.html:396-424). The search box on the results filters the week already on screen and nothing else. Someone who knows an artist's name but not their city cannot find them.

### Traps for copy

- THE LANDING PAGE HAS NEVER HEARD OF VIDEO. public/about.html says only 'fans rate the night, post photos, and you reply' (line 609). It never mentions the 30-second clip, the pasted YouTube/Instagram/TikTok link, editing your own post, or taking it back. The clip rewrite landed 2026-09-06 11:57 (commit f80f71d), after about.html's last edit at 2026-09-05 21:59 (commit 9e9fde4).
- 'Post a video' means two different things and both exist. A CLIP is a file uploaded from the phone, up to 30 seconds and 25MB, played inside MySet with its sound (public/community.html:251-255). A LINK is a pasted YouTube/Instagram/TikTok URL — YouTube embeds, the other two are just links out (netlify/functions/_community.mjs:74-102). One post can carry one of each. Do not merge them into one claim.
- MySet does not record video. The clip comes from the phone's own camera or camera roll through a plain file input, and the site's Permissions-Policy blocks camera and microphone outright (netlify.toml:99). Never write 'record a clip in MySet' or 'hit record'.
- Nothing shrinks or trims a clip any more. Before 2026-09-06 the phone re-encoded every clip; that code is deleted (public/community.html:314-341). A clip over 25MB or over 30 seconds is REFUSED with its actual size or length and told to trim it in the phone's own app. Do not promise 'we'll compress it for you'.
- The 30-second limit is not enforced for every file. The server reads the duration out of an MP4's mvhd box only; a WebM, a fragmented MP4, or anything whose duration reads back as Infinity on the phone gets no length check at all (netlify/functions/_video.mjs:143-157, public/community.html:482). The hard, always-true limit is 25MB of bytes. Say 'up to 25MB' if you need a limit that is always true.
- 'Hide, and undo it' is only half true. Hiding a post deletes its photos and its clip permanently (netlify/functions/_community.mjs:295-303). Un-hiding brings back the words, never the pictures. Two design rules carry marketing weight. Second, verification is presented as a five-line CHECKLIST with the specific blocker named in a toast, never a yes/no (public/venue-studio.html:1423-1511). ### Traps for copy

- THE TICK IS A PAID FEATURE. Verification requires a paid plan as check number one (netlify/functions/_verify.mjs:185-205, 300-301). about.html's venue section says 'your page works whether you verify it or not' but never says the tick itself costs $20/month. And the framing matters: paying does not buy the tick, it opens the door to being checked — the other four checks still have to pass.
- THERE IS A $20/MONTH VENUE PRO TIER AND IT IS SELF-SERVE TODAY. about.html says only 'Claim your venue — free'. Stripe Checkout for venue Pro works (netlify/functions/venueadmin.mjs:415-420, netlify/functions/_billing.mjs:52). Two places in the shipped product still say the opposite — public/venue-studio.html:746-749 ('Venue Pro is NOT self-serve — there is no billing behind it') and the verification checklist at public/venue-studio.html:1456-1458 and the toast at :1504 ('Not self-serve yet: message us'). Those are stale strings, not the behaviour. Never repeat them.
- THE PUBLIC PAGE TELLS ARTISTS 'NO WEBSITE NEEDED' AND THAT IS WRONG. public/venue.html:490 says three artists confirming 'does it — no website needed'. The verdict requires the website AND the domain email AND the site naming the venue AND the paid plan AND the three artists (netlify/functions/_verify.mjs:204-205). Vouches alone verify nothing. The no-website route is Perry checking it by hand (netlify/functions/admin.mjs:363-375).
- THE AUTO-FILL MATCHES CITY AND COUNTRY AS EXACT STRINGS. The venue NAME is normalised (netlify/functions/_venues.mjs:197-208) but the city and country are compared with === (netlify/functions/venue.mjs:84) against a city index keyed on the raw typed strings (netlify/functions/_events.mjs:174-175). 'Koh Phangan' and 'koh phangan' are different towns to this code. That is why the Studio says the city has to match how artists write it (public/venue-studio.html:1105) — do not write copy implying it is fuzzy or forgiving.
- THE AUTO-FILL NEEDS ALL THREE OF NAME, CITY AND COUNTRY OR IT RETURNS NOTHING AT ALL (netlify/functions/venue.mjs:71). A venue that claims a page and stops before filling in the country sees an empty what's-on and no explanation on the public page.
- VENUE MERCH DOES NOT APPEAR ON THE VENUE PAGE. It only appears on /v/<slug>/community (public/community.html:222-231). public/venue.html renders no merch section at all, even though the API sends it (netlify/functions/_venues.mjs:430).
- 'WHO PLAYS HERE' ONLY APPEARS IF MORE THAN ONE ACT MATCHES (public/venue.html:445). A venue with exactly one regular act gets no such section.
- A VENUE WITH NO EVENTS OF ITS OWN IS NOT CLICKABLE IN THE CITY FEED. On public/index.html the venue name is plain text in the 'where' line and the whole row links to the ARTIST's page (public/index.html:467,473; netlify/functions/events.mjs:83-84). Only a venue-run event produces a row that links to /v/<slug>. So 'locals looking for something to do find you' is true through your own events, and only indirectly through artists' gigs.
- THERE IS NO PUBLIC VENUE DIRECTORY. /venues is the Venue Studio sign-in page (netlify.toml:51-53). Nobody browses a list of bars on MySet.
- 'ADD A MANAGER OR BARMAN' ONLY EVER ADDS A BARMAN. The Studio sends no role, so everyone lands as 'crew' (public/venue-studio.html:1735; netlify/functions/venueauth.mjs:163-167). The roleSet endpoint has no UI and, called directly, applies the change and then returns a 400 'unknown action' because it is missing from the allow-list at netlify/functions/venueauth.mjs:223. This is the live machinery of a gig: one Netlify Blobs document per artist holding the show state, twelve sharded fan documents holding every vote, one read-only endpoint (/api/show) that every phone in the room polls, one write endpoint (/api/vote), and the lifecycle module that starts and ends a night — by the artist's tap or by the calendar, with no request behind it. Fans are anonymous (a random id in localStorage, no sign-in); the artist drives it from the Studio's Live tab, which polls every 4 seconds. The defining design decision on this surface is that a plan's audience number is a BILLING line and not a turnstile: a room over its ceiling is never closed and never refuses a vote — it slows its own polling and shortens the board instead. Feature flags are a separate, tiny global document read on the hot poll and cached 60 seconds in module scope; there are exactly two flags and both default ON.

**Structure.** Three surfaces sit on one engine. (1) The fan's voting page at /:slug/vote (public/vote.html): a header with the artist and a credit pill, "Playing now" hero with a Lyrics button, an open/paused strip, "Up next" (top 3 by votes), an optional request/birthday card, search + sort + genre chips, the main votable list, an "Already played" list at replay price, and a bottom dock with More votes / Tip. Tapping a song opens a bottom sheet with a quantity stepper and a plain-English rules list; Confirm casts. (2) The artist's Live tab in the Studio (public/studio.html:1870-1948): a Voting Open/Paused toggle, three stat tiles (votes now, voting/in-room + networks, tips), Now playing with a private chart button, the requests panel, Start/End the show, "Start top voted — <title> (<votes>)", a queue of up to 12 with per-row Start, and Played with Reset votes. (3) The server: /api/show returns everything the room sees plus two self-tuning dials (nextPollMs, board) and the flags in force; /api/vote takes a cast. A fourth surface is gone — public/stage.html is now a 9-line meta-refresh to /studio.html (public/stage.html:5) and has no route in netlify.toml.

**Design.** The fan page is a single self-contained HTML file, no framework, no external fonts or scripts (CSP is default-src 'self', netlify.toml:123). Dark, iOS-flavoured: rounded cards, a red-pink accent, a skeleton shimmer on first paint (public/vote.html:307-312), a "rise" entrance once (public/vote.html:685), haptic buzz on every meaningful tap (public/vote.html:292), a credit pill that bumps when the number changes (public/vote.html:697-699), confetti + a triple buzz on a successful purchase (public/vote.html:966-977), dancing equaliser bars on the Playing-now card. Everything destructive or costly happens in a bottom sheet with a grab-handle drag-to-dismiss and Escape-to-close (public/vote.html:860-870). Pull-to-refresh is a shared script used by all seven pages (public/pull.js) because an installed home-screen app has no address bar. The tone throughout is plainly written and non-technical: refusals read "That one isn't on tonight's list" and "Those votes are cast — they stay with the song" (curly apostrophes are deliberate — they are the app's own copyright fingerprint, netlify/functions/_canary.mjs:26-28). The engine's pacing is invisible by design: at an ordinary gig the board moves within about three seconds and nothing on screen mentions polling at all.

### Traps for copy

- VOTES ARE FINAL, AND THE LANDING PAGE'S OWN DEMO SAYS OTHERWISE. voteFinal defaults to TRUE (netlify/functions/_flags.mjs:34) — a fan cannot take a vote back; the sheet says 'Once you confirm, that's final' (public/vote.html:448). But the interactive demo on /about lets you tap a Voted button to un-vote and get the credit back (public/about.html:768). Either the demo or the copy is wrong, and the demo is the one that contradicts production.
- 'REAL-TIME' AND 'INSTANT' ARE BOTH WRONG. There are no websockets and no server-sent events anywhere in this repo. The board is polled: 3 seconds when things are moving, easing to 10, then 25, then 60 (public/vote.html:1098-1105). Safe phrasings: 'updates itself every few seconds', 'the queue moves while you watch'. Unsafe: real-time, live sync, instantly, push.
- IN A BIG ROOM A POCKETED PHONE CAN GO MINUTES BETWEEN REFRESHES. The rungs are multiples of the server floor, so at a 3,000+ room (floor 20s) the terminal rung is floor x 20 = 400 seconds — about six and a half minutes (public/vote.html:1105). Any tap or screen-on wakes it instantly, but 'never more than a few seconds out of date' is false at scale.
- THE ROOM CEILING IS A BILLING LINE, NOT A DOOR. Free 200 / Plus 1,000 / Pro 2,000 (netlify/functions/_plan.mjs:63, :88, :106). Going over refuses nobody and blocks no vote — there is deliberately no head-count check in the vote path (netlify/functions/vote.mjs:81-92). Never write 'up to 200 people can vote' or 'your room is limited to'. Write 'the night still runs; it just refreshes slower and shows the top of the board'.
- NOTHING ACTUALLY TELLS THE ARTIST THEY WENT OVER. The code comments and tests both say the artist 'is told afterwards', and /api/show emits room.over (netlify/functions/show.mjs:168) — but no page reads it. The Studio's Live tab shows in-room counts only (public/studio.html:1882). Do not promise a notification, a report, or an after-the-gig warning about room size.
- THE ROOM SIZE IS FIXED WHEN THE SHOW STARTS. Upgrading mid-set does not enlarge tonight's room; the plan's number is stamped onto the show record at start (netlify/functions/_lifecycle.mjs:143-147). And a show that began before room caps existed is simply uncapped forever.
- THE SHORT BOARD ONLY TRUNCATES THE UNPLAYED LIST. The 'Already played' section is built separately and is never limited (netlify/functions/show.mjs:100-105), so in a huge room the played list is still complete while the votable list is 15 long. Do not say 'we show the top 15 songs'.
- AUTO-END IS THREE HOURS AFTER THE CALENDAR GIG'S SCHEDULED END, NOT AFTER YOU STOP PLAYING (netlify/functions/_auto.mjs:31, :149). Gigs default to 180 minutes long (netlify/functions/_events.mjs:40), so a 8pm gig with no duration set auto-ends around 2am. It is deferred if a song started in the last 45 minutes, and there is a six-hour hard backstop.
- AUTO-START CAN BE UP TO TWO MINUTES LATE. The cron rings every two minutes (netlify/functions/autocron.mjs:95). Say 'within a couple of minutes of your gig's start time', not 'exactly at'.
- AUTO-START WON'T FIRE AT ALL IF NO SONGS ARE SWITCHED ON, if the artist turned auto-start off, if the free gig cap is spent, or if the artist already started and ended tonight by hand (netlify/functions/_auto.mjs:133-145). It is a helper, not a guarantee.
- THE FREE PLAN'S SHOW CAP IS PER CALENDAR MONTH IN UTC, RESETTING ON THE 1st (netlify/functions/_lib.mjs:131, netlify/functions/_plan.mjs:21-33). It is four a month, not one a week — it was briefly weekly on 2026-09-03 and changed straight back, so older docs may say otherwise.
- 'SET YOUR OWN VOTE RULES' IS A PLUS FEATURE, BUT THE DEFAULTS ARE FREE. A free artist runs on 5 free votes, a 5-vote replay price and the standard packs, and the ROOM's experience is identical (netlify/functions/admin.mjs:1699-1709). Only the artist's ability to change the numbers is gated. Never imply a free artist's audience gets a worse night.
- REQUESTS AND BIRTHDAY SHOUT-OUTS ARE OFF BY DEFAULT (netlify/functions/_lib.mjs:180-183). The buttons are not rendered at all until the artist switches them on. Don't show them in a screenshot captioned as the default fan experience.
- REQUESTS COST VOTES, NEVER MONEY, and a declined request refunds automatically — but ONLY within the same show. A request from an earlier show is deliberately not refunded, because its credits already refreshed (netlify/functions/_requests.mjs:167-177).
- THERE IS NO ENCORE FEATURE. The only 'Encore!' in the app is the label on the $20 tip tile (public/vote.html:937). Every other use of the word is metaphor in code comments.
- THERE IS NO STAGE OR BIG-SCREEN VIEW. public/stage.html redirects to the Studio (public/stage.html:5) and has no route. Never mention projecting the queue on a screen behind the band.
- FANS INSTALL NOTHING AND SIGN IN TO NOWHERE — but that means identity is a random id in the browser's own storage (public/vote.html:287-290). Clear the storage, use a private window, or switch phone, and the votes and any bought credits are gone. Don't promise votes 'follow you'.
- THERE IS NO OFFLINE MODE AND NO QUEUED-VOTE-WHEN-BACK-ONLINE. A failed poll is swallowed silently with no banner (public/vote.html:325); a failed vote retries once then says 'Couldn't reach the room — try again' (public/vote.html:533-534). The service worker deliberately never caches anything under /api (public/sw.js:10-12).
- A SHOW IN 'pre' STATE IS INCONSISTENT: the fan page greys out voting (public/vote.html:574-575), but the server will actually accept a vote — /api/vote only refuses when the show is 'ended' or the window is shut (netlify/functions/vote.mjs:46, :80). Requests, by contrast, do check for 'live' (netlify/functions/_requests.mjs:70). Don't write 'nothing can be voted until you go live'.
- THE FAN CAN STACK UP TO 20 VOTES ON ONE SONG IN ONE CONFIRM (public/vote.html:427-428); the server's hard ceiling is 50 (netlify/functions/vote.mjs:16). The landing demo's one-tap-one-vote model understates the product.
- 'FREE VOTES COME BACK EVERY TIME A NEW SONG STARTS' IS EXACTLY TRUE — but so does the artist's manual 'Reset votes', and so does changing the free-vote count or the replay price, both of which wipe the round on purpose (netlify/functions/admin.mjs:1834, :1949). The fan sees credits reappear without a song having started.
- PUSH NOTIFICATIONS ARE ARTIST-ONLY AND REQUIRE THE STUDIO TO BE INSTALLED. The audience is never pushed to (public/sw.js:31-36). Don't say 'we'll ping your fans'.
- FOUR PLAN FLAGS ARE DESIGNED AND NOT BUILT — promote, analytics, presskit, branding (netlify/functions/_plan.mjs:130). Anything on a pricing table describing earnings-by-venue analytics, a press kit, custom branding or cross-city promotion is describing something with no code behind it. (The separate $10 Featured-shows purchase IS built and IS live, behind the featuredShows flag — do not confuse it with the unbuilt `promote` tier flag.)
- BOTH FEATURE FLAGS DEFAULT ON AND ONLY THE PLATFORM OWNER CAN FLIP THEM (netlify/functions/admin.mjs:176, :190). They are not artist settings and must never be described as customer-facing options. A flip also takes up to 60 seconds to reach every warm server (netlify/functions/_flags.mjs:67).
- THE PROOF NUMBERS ON /about ARE ONE PUB NIGHT — 8 people, 21 votes (public/about.html:486-489). The show engine records the whole round, not just the winner (netlify/functions/admin.mjs:1748-1753), so richer numbers exist, but nothing bigger has been played. Don't extrapolate.

This is MySet's memory of a gig. When a show ends (or a new one starts, or the calendar ends it), `archiveShow` freezes one document per night — songs played in order with their votes, everything the room asked for and never got, how many phones were in the room, how many networks they were on, how many people voted, and what the night took in card payments read straight from Stripe (netlify/functions/_history.mjs:83-241). The sheet is fully built and completely OFF in production — its three env vars have never been set (verified against Netlify: production holds only ADMIN_CODE, AUTH_FROM, RESEND_API_KEY, STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET). **Structure.** Five places a number appears, from most private to most public.

Stripe Billing shipped 2026-09-04 (commit 6fae274): planCheckout, planFinish, planChange, planPortal, planInvoices, planSync and a retention offer, all in admin.mjs:79-113 over _billing.mjs. ## The app shell — PWA/install, service worker, offline, pull-to-refresh, performance, security headers and SEO

**Files:** `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/sw.js`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/pull.js`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/manifest.webmanifest`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/manifest-studio.webmanifest`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/manifest-venue.webmanifest`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/app.css`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/lock.css`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/icons/icon-512.png`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/icons/icon-maskable-512.png`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/vendor/chart.umd.min.js`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/vendor/model-fonts.css`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/vendor/fonts/`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/.well-known/security.txt`, `/Users/perryidyll/Docs/MySet/public/index.html`

MySet has no native app and no build step: eight hand-written single-file HTML pages sit on Netlify's CDN, share exactly two external files (`/app.css` 3.4 KB gzipped and `/pull.js` 2.8 KB gzipped), and talk to Netlify Functions over `/api`. On top of that sits a deliberately timid PWA layer — three web manifests (fan / Artist Studio / Venue Studio), one service worker registered by every page, and one shared pull-to-refresh gesture — plus a security-header block in `netlify.toml` that is unusually strict for a site this small. The audience never installs anything: a fan scans a QR, a web page opens, and the voting page is ~31 KB gzipped in total. Installing is offered only to artists and venues (for home-screen launch and iPhone push alerts) and, oddly, to fans on the public what's-on home page. The whole surface is engineered around one scenario — a cheap phone on bad bar wifi at 10pm — which is why navigations are network-first, nothing is precached, the poll rate backs off, and there are two "if it looks broken" escape hatches in the Artist Studio.

**Structure.** There is no shell in the SPA sense — every page is a standalone document that boots itself. Entry points: `myset.vip/` (index.html, what's-on home, carries the install banner), `/about` (about.html, the landing page), `/:slug` (artist.html), `/:slug/vote` (vote.html), `/:slug/community` and `/v/:slug/community` (community.html), `/v/:slug` (venue.html), `/studio` and `/signup` (studio.html), `/venues` (venue-studio.html), and `/moneymodel` (a passcode-gated function, netlify.toml:23-30). `/stage.html` is now only a meta-refresh redirect to the Studio (public/stage.html:5-9) — a dead surface. Real files always beat the pretty-URL rules, and the `/:slug` catch-all sits last so `/moneymodel` and `/about` are not swallowed by it (netlify.toml:23-79). Every page carries: a `<link rel=manifest>`, an inline SVG favicon data-URI, a `<script src="/pull.js">` loaded blocking, and a late non-blocking `navigator.serviceWorker.register('/sw.js')` after `load`. The three public app pages (artist, venue, community) additionally paint a black `html{background:#0D0B0C}` from an inline style before any external file and then swap `app.css` in via `rel=preload`; both Studios paint `html{background:#000}` and load no `app.css` at all. index.html, about.html and vote.html still load `app.css` render-blocking. The Studios show an animated boot screen with rotating lines and a hard failsafe (studio.html:4244 at 6s, venue-studio.html:593 at 7s), and paint a full-screen "leave" splash on every internal link so a navigation never flashes white (studio.html:4211-4233).

**Design.** One accent (#FF375F → #FF6B45 gradient), an Apple-family system font stack, soft shadows and a spring easing curve, all defined as CSS custom properties in app.css:5-39. Public pages follow the phone's light/dark setting (app.css:40-51, plus per-scheme `theme-color` metas); both Studios are always dark by choice and have no `prefers-color-scheme` block anywhere, which is why the locked-feature veil in lock.css is hard-coded dark (lock.css:26-33). `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured globally (app.css:190-193), in the pull spinner (pull.js:41), in the boot bars, and in the landing page's scroll reveals (about.html:268-270). The brand mark is three bars of unequal height — used as the favicon, the app icon, the intro animation on the home page, and the boot animation in both Studios. The landing page's reveal animations are scoped to a `.anim` class added by an inline script (about.html:36, 263-267), so if JavaScript never runs the content is visible rather than stuck at `opacity:0`. Locked premium features are shown greyed with a pill over them rather than hidden, with two honest states — `.lock` (a higher plan turns it on, tappable) and `.lock.soon` (designed and not built, not tappable on any plan including Pro) — lock.css:20-33.

### Traps for copy

- "Nothing for your fans to install" is true of VOTING and not of the site. The public home page at myset.vip actively offers a fan an install banner and a footer 'Add to home screen' link (public/index.html:273, 279, 508-546), and vote.html/artist.html/venue.html/community.html all link the fan web-app manifest (public/manifest.webmanifest). The honest form is: nothing to install to vote — a fan scans and taps; a fan CAN save MySet to their home screen if they want to.
- MySet does not work offline. The service worker's offline fallback literally says 'MySet needs the internet for the live bits' (public/sw.js:99-105). Nothing is precached (public/sw.js:17-20 and INVARIANT 0ax at INVARIANTS.md:974-979), and /api is never cached (public/sw.js:89). The only offline behaviour is a page you already loaded coming back from cache with a dead board.
- "The page is tiny" is true and provable — vote.html is 24.5 KB gzipped, plus app.css 3.4 KB and pull.js 2.8 KB, so about 31 KB — but the Artist Studio is 81.5 KB gzipped (274 KB raw, 4,263 lines) in ONE file. Do not let a 'tiny' claim be read as covering the artist's own tool.
- PERFORMANCE-PLAN.md:53 says the Studio is '197 KB raw, ~45 KB gzipped'. It was already 211 KB / 63 KB when that line was written and is now 274 KB / 81.5 KB. Do not quote any number out of that document.
- "It backs off politely when the room goes quiet" (about.html:686) describes the polling ladder, which was rewritten on 2026-09-05 at 22:39 — forty minutes AFTER the landing page's last edit. The rungs are now 3s / 10s / 25s / 60s with ±20% jitter, the floor is set by the server based on room size (3s under 200 people, up to 20s past 3,000), and above the default floor another person's vote no longer wakes your phone (public/vote.html:1046-1123). The claim survives; the mechanism behind it is newer than the sentence.
- "If a phone drops out it picks up where it left off" is true of state — the fan id lives in localStorage (vote.html:287-290), a failed vote is retried once with the same idempotent cast id (vote.html:523-535), and an unconfirmed payment is re-claimed for 26 hours (vote.html:1013-1018). It is NOT true of feedback: there is no offline banner or stale indicator anywhere on the voting page. The board just stops moving silently.
- "Everyone's on their own phone and their own data, so you're not sharing one connection" (about.html:685) is a claim about the venue, not about MySet. Nothing in the code makes phones avoid the venue wifi. What IS in the code is that each phone talks only to Netlify's CDN over HTTPS, with HSTS preloaded (netlify.toml:64-68).
- Alerts on the artist's phone are real Web Push and free on every plan — but they depend on VAPID keys being set as Netlify environment variables (netlify/functions/admin.mjs:1637-1642). If they are not, the Studio says 'Not switched on yet'. This repo cannot tell you whether they are set in production; check before claiming it works.
- iPhone alerts do not work in a browser tab at all. The Studio must be added to the home screen first, and the code refuses to even offer the button otherwise (public/studio.html:3140-3141, 3152, 3195-3197). Any 'know the moment someone requests a song' line needs that condition attached for iPhone.
- The Venue Studio gets none of the Artist Studio's shell extras: no push notifications, no 'Reload the Studio', no 'Clear what's stored on this phone'. It has the manifest, the service worker and pull-to-refresh, and that is all (public/venue-studio.html has no match for Notification/PushManager/hardReset/location.reload).
- There is no stage or big-screen display. public/stage.html is a 605-byte meta-refresh to the Studio (public/stage.html:1-9) and has not been meaningfully edited since 2026-08-17. Do not describe a projector or TV view.
- 'Branding — your colours and logo on the audience pages' appears on the Pro plan card but is in NOT_BUILT (netlify/functions/_plan.mjs:130). It is greyed as 'coming' even for Pro. There is no white-label or themed fan page.
- An artist sharing their own MySet link gets a generic preview: og:title 'MySet', og:description 'Live music. You pick what gets played.' and og:image /img/band.jpg — a relative path to a stock band photo (public/artist.html:21-23). The artist's real name is only set later by JavaScript (artist.html:495), which social scrapers do not run. Do not show a mocked-up rich link preview as if it is what MySet produces today.
- Every app page ships an empty <div id="app"></div> and renders client-side (public/artist.html:249). There is no robots.txt, no sitemap.xml and no canonical URL anywhere except the dead stage.html. Do not claim SEO benefits, discoverability, or 'your page shows up in search'.
- The security headers in netlify.toml apply to the static pages in public/ only. Function responses do not receive them — moneymodel.mjs restates the whole CSP itself and says so in a comment (netlify/functions/moneymodel.mjs:69-81). 'Every page is protected by the policy' is not quite right.
- The CSP explicitly does NOT stop an injected inline script — script-src still carries 'unsafe-inline' because every page is one self-contained file (netlify.toml:88-98, INVARIANTS.md:1579-1582). What it stops is that script loading anything or sending anything out. Copy that says 'blocks malicious scripts' overstates it.
- INVARIANT 0cl (INVARIANTS.md:1311-1315) says 'Video is a link, never an upload.' That is now false: as of 2026-09-06 fans upload real video clips up to 25MB and 30 seconds, chunked in 4MB pieces (netlify/functions/clipup.mjs:25-146, netlify/functions/_video.mjs:63-66). The invariants file has not caught up. Trust the code.
- Netlify's Image CDN resizing is on the ARTIST page only — one line, public/artist.html:284. venue.html, community.html and index.html serve the original bytes. Do not claim site-wide automatic image optimisation.
- Room size is not a hard limit and must never be described as one. Free 200 / Plus 1,000 / Pro 2,000 (netlify/functions/_plan.mjs:63, 88, 106) is a billing line: over it the room slows and the board shortens, nobody is refused a vote, and the artist is told afterwards (netlify/functions/show.mjs:32-46, test/roomsize.mjs:1-16). Pro was cut from 3,000 to 2,000 in the same commit that last touched the landing page.
- Past about 200 phones a fan stops seeing the whole setlist — the board is trimmed to the top 40/25/15 (netlify/functions/_lib.mjs:865-871) with an on-screen note. Their own voted songs always survive the cut. If copy promises 'they see your whole setlist', add 'in a normal room'.
- The three-bars logo used in the favicon, the app icon, the intro animation and both boot screens is the same mark. Only two icon files exist, both 512×512 (public/icons/). There is no 192px icon, no monochrome badge, and the installed fan app's splash background is hard-coded light grey (#F5F5F7, public/manifest.webmanifest:10) even on a dark-mode phone.
- The installed apps are locked to portrait (all three manifests, line 9). Do not show a landscape tablet mock-up of the Studio.

That includes /moneymodel, which the function marks 'private, no-store' (netlify/functions/moneymodel.mjs:75 vs public/sw.js:96). Not a fan-facing issue, but do not describe the worker as 'respecting no-store'.
