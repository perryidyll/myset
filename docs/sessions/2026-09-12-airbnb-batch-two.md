# 2026-09-12 — the second batch from the Airbnb design dive

Context: the founder handed over the rest of the dive's integration points — I-05 through I-29, with I-22, I-24 and I-30 left out — and, after a first pass, five rounds of feedback on what came back (the artist pill and carousel, the Studio bars, the front door, the vote page, the venue page). The work ran as one orchestrated session: a read-only survey of every file first (`scratchpad/maps/`), then nine build agents in parallel on disjoint files, then a fresh-context reviewer per file, then a fix agent per file, then the five feedback rounds, then this record. Every edit was exact-match; nothing was committed or pushed by any agent. Another session (Codex) was editing the same checkout throughout and pushed four times in the middle — see "What another session pushed mid-flight".

The rule that ranked the batch: the button tiers and the chip recipe (I-27) had to land first so every other item consumed them instead of inventing another local variant. They did, and then the pages went to production without them — which is the story of this session.
